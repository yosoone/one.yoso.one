# 道場 — The Dojo

> **Cultivation of the heart and harmony.**

---

## One project, three arms

This is one body of work. It has been begun three times under three names, because each attempt could carry only one of its three modes — and each time the parts that would not fit were set down and the survivor was renamed.

The three modes are **study**, **practice**, and **transmission**. They are not stages. They run at once.

The principle that binds them is already in the vault, quoted twice in Yoso's own hand: **Shushō Ittō** (修証一等), Dōgen's oneness and equality of practice and realization.[^1] Zazen is not a means toward a realization that arrives later; sitting _is_ the actualization. Applied here: the study is not preparation for the practice, and the practice is not an illustration of the study. _Shu_ and _shō_ are one and inseparable.

See [[2026-09-02-kokoro-shinsei-dojo-challenge|2026-09-02 — Kokoro, Shinsei, Dojo as overlapping circles]], where the three were first drawn together, and [[2026-09-13-shinsei-visual-practice|2026-09-13 — the soft edge, uncovering rather than building]], where _Shushō Ittō_ arrived.
