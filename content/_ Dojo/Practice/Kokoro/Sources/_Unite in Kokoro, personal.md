United in _kokoro_ (心)

A realisation that body, heart and mind are undivided.

The integration of thoughts, emotions, and the sensations felt within the body; functioning as a whole.

Kokoro is an anchor for those seeking the truth, the truth within, and the truth expressed by those around us. It guides us through emotions, realizing that suffering is a pathway to harmony. Kokoro with patience and open mind becomes an anchor in an ever synchronous flowing reality .

To reach the essence of Kokoro, requires us to cultivate mindfulness, develop our awareness, the presence that helps us inquire the depth of our thoughts and emotions with the purest of intentions.

It encourages to understand and explore meaning, but above all to recognize the interdependence we have with others.

The ability to be totally open and honest with ourselves to gradually reveal our inner world. Each insight, thought, emotion has to be welcome, treated with care, kindness, compassion  and sincerity.

The quiet power of intimacy, meeting in oneself, our inner sanctuary; the sacred garden we cherish and cultivate with love and attention; the integration of thoughts, and emotions within the body.

Refusing emotions and memories is similar to refusing a beggar at the door with fear of looking at their eyes, or hear their stories. Honesty is the chance we have to understand what are and whom we became today; as ‘individuals’, and how we connect as members of society.

Kokoro might reveal an intimate desire for union and the necessary understanding to support each others .

As with empathy, Uniting in kokoro, might mean we carry the burden of others.
A need for conflict resolution, removing frictions, allowing for a natural flow of emotions and energy that does need to create opposition or conflicts, but embrace honour and responsibility.

A commitment to harmony, an acceptance of transience and imperfection.

Honor and integrity.

If Kokoro is lead by compassion, by purifying our hearts and intentions. It can lead us to a deeper understanding and appreciation of consciousness. Merging the ‘spiritual’ to the emotional, energy to the body, symbolism to the rational.

(shinto spirits, purification, limits of consciousness, known and known, visible and invisible).

Kokoro is not simply within, but as human beings it is our responsibility.

\--
Often when the mind is submerged in a culture, information overload, our physicality trapped in systemic dopamine hits — we do not have the time to rest and find clarity . It is by allowing space for kokoro that kokoro can exist.

By Honouring kokoro, it influences how we relate to communities, and how we define interaction with reality. It is recognizing the kokoro in everyone and reveal the essence of a culture.
