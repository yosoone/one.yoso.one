# Trillions of Cells Within

Once we become aware of the multitude of biological and chemical phenomenons that sustains our Human bodies — and by extension, recognise that the same phenomenons apply to other beings, and on a global scale,  — we can choose to honour life and its processes on a cellular and energetic level.

> Breathe in…
> Breathe out…
> The Energy that connects all forms…
> Infinite chains of chemical reactions…
> Trillions of cells living within…
> Who am I?
>
> - Y

# Practice

Breathe in quietness, join your hands at the heart, honour the life within, breathe deeply, let your attention merge with your breath, develop gratitude for the life we have been given, accept the eternal cycle of life, visualise life transform with each breath, cultivate an appreciation for all phenomenons. Embrace the mystery of life.
