# Heart, Body and Intention — Cleaned

> **Cleaned 25 September 2026.** The original Perplexity answer (18 February 2025) was removed. Its sources were mainly Wikipedia, dharma-centre blogs, Reddit and YouTube, and several claims misread the texts. Only points supported by [[Trusted Sources]] remain. The full treatment is in [[Intention]].

## What the verified sources say

- **Intention is the deed.** In AN 6.63 the Buddha identifies karma with intention (_cetanā_): having intended, one acts through body, speech and mind.[^1]
- **Dhammapada 1–2 is ethical, not idealist.** Acting with corrupted intention brings suffering; acting with pure intention brings happiness. The translator reads _mano_ here as the active, intending side of mind — a sequence of intention, action and consequence, not a claim that mind creates the world.[^2]
- **Right intention has three parts:** renunciation, good will and harmlessness (SN 45.8). This is the sutta definition; the removed note wrongly credited it to an Abhidhamma list of six "intentional orientations."[^3]

## Removed claims

The reading of Dhp 1 as _cittamātra_ (mind-only), "vibrational patterns" and "reality-shaping technology," the body as a "holographic projection of consciousness," collective intention influencing matter through quantum entanglement, and unsourced claims about neuroplasticity and default-mode-network findings. The Python meditation timer was also removed.
