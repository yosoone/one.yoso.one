# Intention and the Quantum World — Cleaned

> **Cleaned 25 September 2026.** The original Perplexity answer (1 June 2026) was removed. Its sources were coaching blogs, LinkedIn posts, Reddit, Wikipedia and wellness sites, and several claims were contradicted by the research they cited. Only points supported by [[Trusted Sources]] remain. The full treatment is in [[Intention]], §8.

## What the verified research says

- **The "observer effect" did not involve a human observer.** The 1998 Weizmann Institute experiment showed that coupling electrons to a "which-path" detector suppresses their interference. The observer was a measuring device, not awareness or intention.[^1]
- **Intention and random number generators.** A meta-analysis of 380 studies found a significant but very small overall effect; effect sizes shrank as studies grew larger and varied enormously, a pattern the authors showed publication bias alone could produce.[^2]
- **Where intention's effects are well supported:** through the person — attention, behaviour and practice. See [[Intention]], §§1 and 7.

## Removed claims

Quantum gratitude, the "quantum key," Zero Point Field manifestation, William Tiller's intention experiments, "2–3% above chance" micro-PK effects, a "decline effect" preserving no-signalling, and gamma-wave tunnelling in microtubules. No peer-reviewed support was found for these as stated.
