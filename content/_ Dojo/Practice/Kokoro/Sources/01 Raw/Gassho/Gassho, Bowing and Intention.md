# Gassho + Bow (合掌)

_Transcribed from handwritten note — #kokoro, 240907_

---

> "Remember, you can always inspire / guide someone by sharing your thoughts. You feel young with a lot to learn — but don't forget you are 50!!"

---

- Hands in prayer.
- Re-centre _(KoKoro)_
- Cultivating presence, (awareness), consciousness

**Morning practice →**

- Welcoming the new day with gratitude.

- Accepting that life will bring you the people, events, situations you can best serve — and at the same time, that makes you grow.

- Life is in constant evolution. Change is constant _(only certainty)_. loosing job, changing career, causes, losing loved ones, new borns. Stranger in the present.

---

I find conventional manners getting lost in contemporary Japan — and it would be sad if those manners disappear.\*

> _Cultivating the spirit.. is Somebody you cannot buy — as a practice, as a please and a thank you. It warms the heart to be respected._
> Y-

---

> Respecting others is not a matter of social status or circumstance.
> It is **acknowledging and respecting the life within each one of us**.
> Y-

— Beyond duality! Honouring the space! "Self", "Others", as one flowing energy / Y.

---

_"Be gentle to life… it is a prayer to experience — not a problem to be solved."_ (Transcription bug from my hand written notes, I decide to keep!)

Original Quote

> Life is not a problem to be solved, but a reality to be experienced.
> [Soren Kierkegaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Søren_Kierkegaard)

Quote originally found in a book at Manuela's house when I was leaving. A moment I felt the cool breeze 'spirit'.

Handwritten version from my notebook:

"Be gentle to life… it is a mystery to experience — not a problem to be solved."

**Humility** / Cultivating a deep sense of **humanity** (Humility, original)

**Gratitude**

Showing respect to the _'other'_ —
Life, spirits & spaces

---

While travelling, or at each encounter. JEDI?  — we recognize each other!
You are hugging, holding bags, someone bows to you?
Try it — it gets contagious! Be sincere when you do it. Express the true 'Self' you cultivate.

---

A great way to say thank you to someone, when paying for something: i.e. Seigo, showing gratitude _on top_ of the money you pay, as a transfer of energy and respect. From the heart. Not a transaction.

If you are too shy to be seen bowing— you can visualise it in your heart & mind as an [[Intention]]to the visible & invisible world).

[[Honour Each Cell in Your Body]]

- 🙏 Entities attach to someone…
- 🙏 Deep respect to the person — _"and the spirits attached to it, the 'invisible realm"_

_(Sketches of bowing postures — standing bow, deep bow, seated bow as in prayer)_

---

It all started with **Martial arts, meditation, reiki** — and as you develop your awareness, consciousness, it will become second nature. Also show your respect to the space, as if you were entering a temple or the Dojo.

⇒ Try it for yourself and see how it **transforms you, your relationship to others, and your reality**.

Share the love given — to infinity.
