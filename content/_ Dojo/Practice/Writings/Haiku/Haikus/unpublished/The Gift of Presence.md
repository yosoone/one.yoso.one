" The gift of presence,
as in the bow...
The gift of the bow..
that acknowledges your presence.

The gift of respect,
the gift that honours you.

A free, sacred,
sincere gift.

The gift on the path... "

Y.

---

'The Gift of Presence,
Aware of one's thoughts,
In tune with one's energy,
Present in the moment.

The gift of conscience,
Beyond judgment,
Focused on harmony,
Healing presence.'

Y.

---

Present, mindful now.
Peaceful heart, a quiet mind.
Harmony’s embrace.

Y.
