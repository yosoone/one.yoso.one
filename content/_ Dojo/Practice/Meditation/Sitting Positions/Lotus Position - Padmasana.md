The Goal is to open your hips and gradually improve your flexibility. Do not twist your knees Joints.

# Preparations

- Butterfly - one to two minutes- Ankle Rotation, knee rotation
- Japanese Leg Sitting - Girl Style Both Side
- Knee above 90 sitting, both side (see pics below)
- 90 90 Stretch
- Grab Big Toe, Strecth. Inhale lift your leg up, Exhale bring toe next to the chest (If more advanced, closer to the ear)
- Half Lotus, 3-5 min each side
