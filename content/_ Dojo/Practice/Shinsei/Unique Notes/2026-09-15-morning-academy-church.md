Royal Academy of Arts, Brussels — Painter's heart.
A spiritual – experience[^1]

\~

Another dream occurred: when I went back to the painting studios, in the "master's painter's" spot. One of the studio "lost" going up the stories. My woman was in black and white & it felt like a religious experience.

A privilege, intrigued, but also a place where I belong.

The issue seems that I had "given" up the practice. But the heart and the wisdom were aligned. I have the painter's heart. A mix of "insecurity" maybe, or was it just intrigue?

It has been a few times I have been back to the Academy in my dreams, but this time felt really different in it's frequence, as if "the ultimate spiritual experience was being felt through the ceiling."

I do belong there indeed, but deepening the practice is necessary. I have loved painting so much.

However it seemed that my curiosity and life's evolution has taken me away from it during my time in the UK.

It seems that tattooing, and the Japanese visual arts have brought back that love.

At this current time tho, it is not only a visual exploration or raw expression, but clearly a means to pursue and express the spiritual path. (I guess it had always been the case, it has just become clearer.)

One note on matters — If I'd live the textural, material, tactile aspect of painting. The current process seems to clearly use Japanese papers, inks, softer tones and colours.

Let's explore, express, document, reveal the symbolism.

Play, reveal.

Go deeper, feel lighter.
This is your rebirth.

P.S. You remember talking to me? Get in the mood, open your crown chakra.

Yoso, Miyazaki
