Shinsei Visual Practice

As I am still spent day out, a wide net of research, attempting to describe the experience I want to share with others.

The Shinsei series brings me back to the origin of the emotion. The reduction of the "mind", complete, when are fixed refuge.

Beyond analysis is experience.
Beyond the idea of an idea is energy!
