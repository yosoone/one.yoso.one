# SHINSEI 心性 — Mindful Creation

_Works and Practice by Yoso · Soul Carver · Reiki Master · Lay Monk · Miyazaki_

---

Shinsei (心性), mindful creation, is the spirit moving through every work here. Each brushstroke, each drop of ink and water, each carved line arrives as meditation. Across tattooing and Irezumi, Sumi-e, haiku, and photography, Yoso pursues one inquiry: the cultivation of consciousness and a living harmony with the world. In Japan, tattooing reveals itself as the practice gathering everything together — craft, healing, and meditation, each line a commitment.

His guiding koan, carved into the practice: _Is this pure?_ Pure as alignment with the source — clear, whole, true to love. The teachings of Dzongsar Khyentse Rinpoche and the Bodhisattva vow orient his heart toward the benefit of all beings. Together these form an ever-growing mandala of activities — tattooing, Reiki, painting, writing, teaching — each petal opening from the same centre.

_The heart of the work resides in the cultivation of light._ One breath at a time. Gassho. — Y.
