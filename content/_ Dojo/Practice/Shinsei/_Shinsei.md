### Rothko

#### The Painting vibrates with a life of it's own

It is a widely accepted notion among painters that it does not matter what one paints as long as it is well painted. There is no such thing as good painting about nothing. We assert that the subject is crucial and only that subject matter is valid, which is tragic and timeless. That is why we profess spiritual kinship with primitive and archaic [Art](https://lab.marconoris.com/Arte/Arte). We demand from the viewer a sensitivity which we ourselves have had to acquire. It is a question of sensitizing one's self to the point where the paintings vibrate with a life of their own."[ \[1\]](https://lab.marconoris.com/#fn-1-2c82c29fe78db724)

-Mark Rothko

### Rubin

#### Intention

“Our thoughts, feelings, processes, and unconscious beliefs have an energy that is hidden in the work. This unseen, unmeasurable force gives each piece its magnetism. A completed project is only made up of our intention and our experiments around it. Remove intention and all that’s left is the ornamental shell.” ([Rubin and Strauss, 2023, p. 60](zotero://select/library/items/DE4MSIPA)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KBTYMMWH?page=60\&annotation=LPKASXXQ))

“It is not an exercise of thought, a goal to be set, or a means of commodification. It is a truth that lives inside you. Through your living it, that truth becomes embedded in the work. If the work doesn’t represent who you are and what you’re living, how can it hold an energetic charge? An intention is more than a conscious purpose, it’s the congruence of that purpose. It requires an alignment of all aspects of one’s self. Of conscious thought and unconscious beliefs, of capabilities and commitment, of actions when work” ([Rubin and Strauss, 2023, p. 61](zotero://select/library/items/DE4MSIPA)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KBTYMMWH?page=61\&annotation=VLJDEWIP))
