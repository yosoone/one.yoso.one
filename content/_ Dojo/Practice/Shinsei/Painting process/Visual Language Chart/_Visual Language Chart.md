## How Each Section Reveals Original Mind

| Section | Original mind |
|---|---|
| **Brushstroke** | The uncorrected stroke is mind before editing |
| **Ink tone** | One black holds every tone, as one mind holds every state |
| **Water** | Water has no fixed form of its own |
| **Absorbent washi** | Blank paper is the original emptiness every mark appears on |
| **Spray, mist, rain** | What is veiled is present, just not yet seen |
| **Sized paper and silk** | A clear surface that reflects what is placed on it |
| **Gold and indigo** | Light appearing out of darkness |
| **_Yohaku_ and _ma_** | The empty paper is original mind; the marks appear in it |
| **The scroll** | A still axis to return to, where the eye comes to rest |
| **Seal or no seal** | The self made small, or no self at all |

_The work doesn't create original mind; it clears a space where it can be seen._[^2]
