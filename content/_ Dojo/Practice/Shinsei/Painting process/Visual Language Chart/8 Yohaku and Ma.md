# _Yohaku_ and _Ma_ (Empty Space)

**Shinsei (心性, read _shinshō_): original mind**, "the unchanging essence of mind."[^1] Every entry ends with one question: _**how does this reveal original mind?**_

**Core idea:** in East Asian composition the empty part of the surface is **not background but the larger half of the work**. _Yohaku_ (余白, "remaining white") is the unpainted space. _Ma_ (間) is the interval between things, in space or in time, that lets them relate.[^2] Laozi's image is the wheel, the pot and the room: their use comes from what is not there.[^3]

## Kinds of empty space

| Kind | Reading | What it is | Carries | Use when | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Remaining white** 余白 | _yohaku_ | Paper left unpainted | Openness, breath, possibility | Always; the default ground | The original emptiness |
| **Interval** 間 | _ma_ | The gap between two forms | Relationship, tension, silence | Between marks that should "speak" to each other | Awareness between thoughts |
| **Time interval** | _ma_ | The pause between strokes | Rhythm, breath | In the act of painting | The still point before action |
| **Mist space** | _yohaku_ as atmosphere | Forms fading into blank paper | Depth, veiling | Landscapes, fading forms | Present but unseen[^4] |
| **Directional space** | — | Open space in front of a form's movement or gaze | Direction, expectation | Where a stroke or form "moves" | Where the mind is heading |
| **Enclosed space** | — | Emptiness held inside a form (the inside of an _ensō_) | Containment, home | Circles, vessels | Emptiness held, not lost |

## Asymmetry

| Principle | Reading | What it does | Tradition | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Asymmetry** 不均整 | _fukinsei_ | Off-centre balance; nothing mirrored | First of Hisamatsu's seven Zen art qualities[^5] | Mind not fixed to a centre |
| **Simplicity** 簡素 | _kanso_ | Only what is needed | Hisamatsu[^5] | Nothing added |
| **Stillness** 静寂 | _seijaku_ | Calm, silent space | Hisamatsu[^5] | Rest |
| **One-corner composition** | 馬一角 (_Ba Ikkaku_, "one-corner Ma") | Weight in one corner, the rest left open | Southern Song painters Ma Yuan and Xia Gui; taken up in Muromachi Japan[^6] | The small self before the vast |
| **The unfinished** | — | The viewer completes the work | Okakura's _Book of Tea_[^7] | The work continues in the viewer's mind |

## Proportion (practical guide)

| Share of empty space | Effect | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Little** (mostly painted) | Dense, full, present | Works about energy or abundance |
| **About half** | Balanced dialogue | Most works |
| **Most** (a few marks) | Vast, silent, contemplative | Works about stillness and original mind |
| **Almost all** (one mark) | The mark becomes a point in infinity | _Ensō_, single strokes |

These are working guides, not rules from a source.

## References in art

| Work | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Hasegawa Tōhaku, _Pine Trees_ | Forms appearing and vanishing in empty mist[^4] |
| Ma Yuan, Southern Song album leaves | One-corner composition[^6] |
| Arata Isozaki, _MA: Space-Time in Japan_ (1978–79) | _Ma_ presented as a unified idea of space and time[^2] |
| Kenya Hara, _White_ | Emptiness as a receptive state waiting to be filled[^8] |

## Meaning

The Heart Sutra's "form is emptiness, emptiness is form" (色即是空　空即是色) gives the deepest reading:[^9] the marks and the empty paper are not two things. In Shinsei terms, the empty paper **is** original mind, and the marks are what appears in it.

**Statements:** "The empty part is the larger half of the work." · "_Ma_ is the pause where relation happens." · "The painting ends in the viewer's mind."[^7]
