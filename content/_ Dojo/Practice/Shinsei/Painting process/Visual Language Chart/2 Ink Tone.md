# Ink Tone (_bokushoku_ 墨色)

**Shinsei (心性, read _shinshō_): original mind**, "the unchanging essence of mind."[^1] Every entry ends with one question: _**how does this reveal original mind?**_

**Core idea:** "ink has five colours."[^2] Tone is **distance** — in space, time and feeling.

| Tone | Reading | Carries | Use when | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Scorched** 焦墨 | _shōboku_ | Intensity, essence | Final accents | Nothing diluted |
| **Dense** 濃墨 | _nōboku_ | Presence, foreground | Main forms | Here and now |
| **Heavy** 重墨 | _jūboku_ | Substance, support | Middle ground | What holds |
| **Pale** 淡墨 / 薄墨 | _tanboku_ / _usuzumi_ | Distance, grief, impermanence | Mist, fading | Letting go[^3] |
| **Clear** 清墨 | _seiboku_ | Air, void | Ground washes | Almost nothing; the paper speaks |

| Technique | Reading | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| **Broken ink** 破墨 | _haboku_ | One state dissolving into another |
| **Splashed ink** 溌墨 | _hatsuboku_ | Chaos becoming form[^4] |
| **Accumulated ink** 積墨 | _sekiboku_ | Time, patience |
| **Stale ink** 宿墨 | _shukuboku_ | Residue of the past[^5] |

| Ink stick | Diluted look | Pairs with |
|---|---|---|
| **Pine soot** 松煙墨 | Cool blue-grey | Indigo, _usuzumi_ |
| **Oil soot** 油煙墨 | Warm brown-black | Gold, foreground |
| **Bluish** 青墨 | Cool grey | Indigo[^6] |
| **Brownish** 茶墨 | Sepia-grey | Gold, aged paper |

**Statements:** "Tone is distance." · "The dilution is the meaning." · "One black holds every colour."
