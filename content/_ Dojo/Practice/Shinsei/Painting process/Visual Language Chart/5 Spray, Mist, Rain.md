# Spray, Mist, Rain

**Shinsei (心性, read _shinshō_): original mind**, "the unchanging essence of mind."[^1] Every entry ends with one question: _**how does this reveal original mind?**_

**Core idea:** a **trace without contact** — made by breath, air or sky. The oldest sprays record an absent hand.[^2]

| Method | Reading | Carries | Use when | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Blank paper as mist** | _yohaku_ | Absence as atmosphere | Veiled forms | Present but unseen[^3] |
| **Haze bands** | _suyarigasumi_ すやり霞 | Time, cuts between scenes | Narrative scrolls | What is hidden still exists[^4] |
| **Mist sprayer** | _kirifuki_ 霧吹き | Breath, humidity | Priming, softening | Breath on the surface |
| **Blown ink** | _fukizumi_ 吹墨 | Particles, snow, stars | Night, scattered energy | Many points, one field[^4] |
| **Gold dust** | _sunago_ 砂子 | Radiance | Sacred grounds | Light diffused[^4] |
| **Flicked spatter** | — | Released energy | Accents | Impulse |
| **Airbrush** | — | Smooth light, but industrial | Grounds, sparingly | Only if justified as light[^5] |
| **Stencil / resist** | — | Presence through absence | Leaves, symbols | The trace of what was there |
| **Real rain** | — | Grace, impermanence | Outdoor works | Received, not made |
| **Painted rain** | — | Sudden change | Line work | Movement[^6] |

**Anchors:** Fujiko Nakaya's fog sculptures[^7] · Tanizaki's _In Praise of Shadows_.[^8]

**Statements:** "Mist is a trace without contact." · "What is veiled is not absent."[^9] · "Rain is a co-author."
