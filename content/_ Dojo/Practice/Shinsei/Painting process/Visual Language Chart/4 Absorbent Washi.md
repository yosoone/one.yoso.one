# Absorbent Washi (生紙 _kigami_)

**Shinsei (心性, read _shinshō_): original mind**, "the unchanging essence of mind."[^1] Every entry ends with one question: _**how does this reveal original mind?**_

**Core idea:** unsized paper takes ink in permanently. Choosing it means choosing **irreversibility**.

| Paper | Absorbency | Character | Use when | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kōzo** 楮 | High, feathered | Strong, organic | Bold gestures, _ensō_ | The living fibre holds the act |
| **Mitsumata** 三椏 | Moderate, even | Smooth, refined | Controlled strokes | Intention stays close |
| **Gampi** 雁皮 | Low | Lustrous, fine | Delicate line | The trace rests lightly |
| **Gasenshi** 画仙紙 | Very high | Very responsive | Splash, bleed | Every hesitation recorded |
| **Sized** 礬水引き | Holds ink on surface | Controllable | Gold, pigment, layering | (See section 6) |

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Front / back** | Back is rougher, more absorbent |
| **Thickness** | Thin: fast, luminous · Thick: rich, weighty |
| **Aged paper** | Often said to bleed more evenly[^2] |
| **Humidity** | Wider bleeds in humid seasons |
| **Mounting** 裏打ち | Needed for scrolls; plan from the start[^2] |

**Statements:** "I choose paper that remembers." · "The empty paper is not empty" — _yohaku_.[^3] · "The material has a living lineage."[^4][^5]
