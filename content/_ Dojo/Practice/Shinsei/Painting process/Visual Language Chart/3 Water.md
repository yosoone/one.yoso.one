# Water (_mizu_ 水)

**Shinsei (心性, read _shinshō_): original mind**, "the unchanging essence of mind."[^1] Every entry ends with one question: _**how does this reveal original mind?**_

**Core idea:** _suiboku_ (水墨) — water comes first. Water decides how the black behaves.[^2][^3]

| State / technique | Reading | Carries | Use when | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Saturated brush** | 潤 _jun_ | Abundance, life | Beginnings | Generosity |
| **Dry brush** | 渇 _katsu_ | Age, effort, void | Endings, emphasis | The bare act |
| **Wet → dry in one stroke** | — | A life cycle | Long strokes, _ensō_ | A lifetime in one gesture |
| **Gradation** | _bokashi_ 暈し | Threshold, dawn/dusk | Horizons | Transition |
| **Pooling-in** | _tarashikomi_ たらし込み | Chance, living surface | Clouds, moss | The material answers[^4] |
| **Boneless** | _mokkotsu_ 没骨 | Form without boundary | Flowers, mist | No fixed edge |
| **Painting around** | _sotoguma_ 外隈 | Presence through absence | Moon, snow, light | The unpainted is the subject[^5] |
| **Grinding water** | 硯水 | Preparation | Before every work | Settling the breath |

**Statements:** "Suiboku means water first." · "Water is the co-author." · "Like water, the work yields."[^2]
