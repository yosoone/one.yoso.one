# Gold and Indigo

**Shinsei (心性, read _shinshō_): original mind**, "the unchanging essence of mind."[^1] Every entry ends with one question: _**how does this reveal original mind?**_

**Core idea:** gold is **not a colour but a behaviour**: it gathers and returns light.[^2] Indigo (or _sumi_) gives it the darkness it needs in order to glow. Together they picture **light appearing out of darkness**.[^3] This note is the chart summary; the research is in [[_On the use of gold]].

## Forms of gold

| Form | Reading | Look | Carries | Use when | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gold leaf** 金箔 | _kinpaku_ | Flat, mirror-like field | Placelessness, radiance | Grounds, large fields | Space without location |
| **Gold ground** 金地 | _kinji_ | Whole surface in gold | Transcendent space[^4] | Rarely; for works outside time | Beyond measure |
| **Cut gold** 截金 | _kirikane_ | Hair-fine lines of leaf | A thread of light that follows form[^5] | Contours, robes, single lines | One clear line of awareness |
| **Gold paint** 金泥 | _kindei_ | Soft, brushable, burnished | Devotion, the Word | Writing, fine marks, sutra-like passages | The trace made luminous |
| **Gold dust** 砂子 | _sunago_ | Scattered glitter | Diffused light | Skies, mist fields | Light everywhere, no source |
| **Gold flakes / strips** 野毛 | _noge_ | Irregular shards | Sparkle, rhythm | Accents | Moments of clarity |
| **Silver** | _gin_ 銀 | Cool, tarnishes to grey-black | Moon, change, time | Alternating with gold[^6] | Light that also ages |

## Five qualities of gold

| Quality | What the metal does | Tradition says | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Luminosity** | Returns light | Light seems to come from within[^7] | The mind's own light |
| **Incorruptibility** | Does not tarnish | Permanence, the deathless[^2] | The unchanging essence |
| **Refinement** | Purified by fire | Pure gold is "pliant, malleable and luminous," like the purified mind[^8] | Practice as removing, not adding |
| **Placelessness** | Beaten into depthless leaf | The gold ground cancels place[^4] | Mind not located anywhere |
| **Preciousness** | Rare, costly | Offering, merit[^2] | Practice as offering |
| _Shadow side_ | Attracts the eye | Idol, vanity, distraction[^2] | Gold that points at itself, not beyond |

## Pairings

| Pairing | Ground | Effect | Tradition | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gold on indigo** 紺紙金泥 | _konshi kindei_ | Warm light on cool night; strongest contrast | Heian decorated sutras[^9] | Light out of darkness |
| **Gold on sumi** | Ink-washed ground | Gold on black; closest to Tibetan soot-coated sutra paper[^3] | Tibetan _mthing shog_ | Ink as the night of the mind |
| **Gold on purple** 紫紙金字 | _shishi kinji_ | Regal, harmonious | Nara state sutras[^3] | (Display rather than stillness) |
| **Gold and silver lines** | Indigo | Warm and cool alternating | _Chūson-ji-kyō_[^3] | Breath: in and out |
| **Gold in shadow** | Dim room, single light | Gold noticed rather than displayed | Tanizaki[^10] | Quiet radiance |

## Using gold with care

| Choice | Reading |
|---|---|
| **A field of gold** | Lifts the whole work out of time and place |
| **A line of gold** | Keeps the work in the world, with one thread of light |
| **Very little gold** | Gold as the result of practice, not decoration |
| **Changing light** | Hang where daylight or candlelight moves, so the work changes like a practice[^7] |

**Statements:** "Gold doesn't shine; it returns light." · "Gold needs darkness." · "The purified mind is pliant, workable and luminous, like gold."[^8]
