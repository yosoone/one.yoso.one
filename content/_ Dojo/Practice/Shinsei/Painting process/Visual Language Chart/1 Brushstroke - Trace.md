# Brushstroke / Trace

**Shinsei (心性, read _shinshō_): original mind**, "the unchanging essence of mind."[^1] Every entry ends with one question: _**how does this reveal original mind?**_

**Core idea:** a stroke is a _trace_, the record of an act, not a picture.[^2] Classical theory makes it the root of painting.[^3][^4]

| Quality | Reading | Carries | Use when | Original mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Centred tip** | _chūhō_ 中鋒 | Presence, centre, weight | Core forms | A settled centre, like posture in zazen |
| **Side tip** | _sokuhō_ 側鋒 | Texture, surface, speed | Secondary forms, contrast | The surface around the centre |
| **Hidden entry** | _zōhō_ 蔵鋒 | Restraint, inward energy | Quiet works | Energy turned inward |
| **Exposed entry** | _rohō_ 露鋒 | Openness, spontaneity | Dynamic marks | Nothing hidden |
| **Flying white** | _hihaku_ 飛白 | Speed, breath, void in form | Letting emptiness breathe | The ground shows through the act[^5] |
| **Bleed** | _nijimi_ 滲み | Softness, letting go | Atmosphere | Control released |
| **Press and lift** | _tonsō_ | Rhythm, pulse | Long strokes | The line breathes |
| **Single stroke** | _ippitsu_ 一筆 | Commitment, truth | Key gestures | Mind before editing |
| **Circle** | _ensō_ 円相 | Wholeness / openness | Signature works | Complete, yet open[^6] |

**Statements:** "The stroke is a trace, not an image." · "The stroke is the person."[^7] · "The stroke is an act in time."[^8]
