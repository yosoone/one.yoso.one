# Series — Gradations of Consciousness

![[_ Dojo/Practice/Shinsei/Artwork Concepts/shinsei-gradations.svg|1100]]

## The Teaching

The zero is one and one is zero. Fullness and emptiness are one consciousness, showing itself across different faces.

Look for the mind within the mind. The series holds three stations of that same mind meeting itself — three gradations, each complete in itself.

## Piece I — Assault

**Visual:** Bold _sumi_ (墨, ink) splash at centre, full saturation. Gold leaf (_kinpaku_, 金箔) gathered along the edges of the paper, where the light collects.

**What it holds:** The mind meeting itself directly, open and unguarded. The assault of arrival. Consciousness as bold presence, undeniable.

**Form:** _Kakejiku_ (掛軸, hanging scroll), vertical. The gesture claims the space.

## Piece II — Soft Grace

**Visual:** Gold particles scattered across the white field, dispersed and fine as dust. The mark releases into light itself.

**What it holds:** Consciousness as diffuse presence, gentled into grace. Fullness appearing as softness, awareness as small particles of luminosity.

**Form:** _Kakejiku_, vertical. The field rests, open and held.

## Piece III — Recognition

**Visual:** The splash and the scattered particles share one composition. The bold mark and the gentle dispersal occupy the same space, inseparable.

**What it holds:** The witness recognising itself. The two faces revealed as one consciousness all along — fullness and emptiness arriving together.

**Form:** _Kakejiku_, vertical. The whole teaching held in a single moment.

## The Series as Teaching

Three scrolls, three faces of one mind. The sequence moves through fullness, into softness, into the recognition that both were always the same.

The zero and the one return to their source: the mind that was looking all along.
