“Enjoying the time to resource, refresh our minds and tune in inner peace” Y.

Our Reiki retreat is offered in South Japan for individuals or small groups that wishes to relax, share, study Reiki and refresh their minds in the Japanese country side. This is an excellent way of resourcing yourself, especially for people who live a busy city lifestyle or who are going to a high level of stress, it  allows for refreshing perspective, experiencing  new ways of life while discovering some aspects of Japanese culture. In the simplicity, near the ocean & surrounded by nature. A return to simplicity & resourcing for some, an awakening for others. Miyazaki is also a famous spot for surfers so if you love surf, you would be in the ideal environment to practice both surf and Reiki.

We can accommodate people who wish to study Reiki practice for a day or for a week. Other activities can be included in the schedule according to what you try to accomplish. Activities can include, day and night meditation, Reiki, chi gong, Japanese cooking, gardening, site seeing, tea ceremony, massage, visits to natural spring among other activities.

We encourage you to mix practice and pleasure, so you should also go and explore by yourself the beautiful Japanese nature and other beautiful spots Kyushu has to offer. A Reiki Retreat can be the perfect companion to those who take the time to travel Japan.

According to the number of people you wish to bring with you, For one or two people, we welcome you in Yoso’s own dojo, you would be sleeping on futon & tatamis in a very simple settings where main meals and beverages will be organised, or we can help organise accommodations according to your desires, such as luxury hotels or guest  post we know that can accommodate more people. While we can provide guidance for travelling to us according to what we know. We are not tour agents so  it will be your responsibility to organise your travels. We can pick you up at the airport or Ferry terminal if this easier for you.

While we also encourage people to visit Kyoto, the benefits of Miyazaki rural settings allows you to live in a very relaxed environment away from the city buzz. While the train journey is quite long if you come from Osaka, Tokyo or Nagoya, travelling by train to us is beautiful and the closer you’ll get to us, the deeper you will get in nature and discover a beautiful side of Japan.

if you have any questions, please get in touch!
