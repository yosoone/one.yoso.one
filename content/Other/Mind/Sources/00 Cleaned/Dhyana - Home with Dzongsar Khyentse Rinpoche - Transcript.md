# Dhyana — Home

_Cleaned transcript of Dzongsar Khyentse Rinpoche's weekly Dhyana session on home — the mind as the only real home, and a guided watching of body, feeling, and references..[^1] Punctuation, paragraphing, and section headings added; filler words, repetitions, and false starts removed. Wording, imagery, and the sequence of instructions follow the spoken original.[^2]_

---

Welcome back, everyone.

_Dhyāna_ (ध्यान, meditative absorption) can be understood in many ways. For now, let's try to understand it as the state in which you are not distracted.

Today we are also going to talk about the idea of **home**. Home is very important, but at the same time it is quite vague. For many, home is where you feel you belong. We also feel that home is where you experience peace — somewhere safe. Supposedly, it is somewhere safe. For some people, it is where your family is.

And this is not only true for human beings. For many beings — animals, ants, bugs, birds — home is important.

Home is where you bring all your shopping back to. It is where you feel at ease; you probably walk around just in your underwear. But many times this is only an ideal. Many times, home is also where everything goes wrong.

Actually, some people feel they have no home. Especially modern people — I think they feel less and less that they have a place called home. A lot of us stay in a rented place. For a lot of people, your own suitcase is almost like a home. And a lot of people feel more at ease with a stranger than with their own family member.

Now, according to the Buddha, ==the real home is the mind that you have==. Your mind is where you always come back, no matter what. But the problem is that most of us are always going out of this home. And outside your home you face all kinds of challenges — and instead of really trying to come back home, we drift further. ^real-home

So today, what we are going to do is try to come back to this home.

---

## Settling

For that, let us just be however you are. Whether you are standing or walking, just be there, without any specific posture. Be there for a few moments.

Try not to entertain yourself. Don't send messages. Don't read your phone. Don't look at a specific object. Just be how you are.

So let's do that for a few moments.

> If you are hearing a dog barking, just listen. If your mind is beginning to wander, thinking a lot of things, just come back to where you are.

This mind that you have is going to be with you all the time. Even your beloved body may change, or it may perish, but mind continues. And this mind that you have is also the most powerful. ==If you know how to be with this mind, this mind is the most peaceful and the safest.== No matter what you face, if you know how to be in this home, you will always find security — some sort of escape. ^safest

---

## Watching the body

So let us now first watch our body, because your body is so connected to your mind. When I say _watch your body_, you don't have to go through every detail. Just notice your body.

Okay — let's do that.

---

## Watching feeling

Next, let's watch our feeling. It can be a physical feeling, such as feeling itchy, or pain — or, more importantly, feelings such as sadness or hunger. That kind of feeling.

So let's watch our feeling.

---

## The house

We are talking about home. So if your body is the roof, the ceiling, the walls, and the floor, then feeling is more like your bed, your furniture, your kitchen.

But in your home there must also be a place where you put your precious things, where you hide things — a safety box, or maybe a bedroom. ==That is your mind.== ^the-house

And there is something else. We have **references**. There is "I'm a man." "She is a woman." "I'm a Buddhist." "He is a Hindu." It doesn't matter — color, shape, nationality, all these different references. This too is a big part of our home.

---

## Watching references

So today, let us watch our references. Whoever you are, if you are meditating right now — if you are a woman, just watch this reference. You call yourself a woman. I'm not asking you to ask _why_ you are a woman. I'm just asking you to watch the notion: woman, man, businessman, doctor, nurse. All kinds of references, whatever references you have.

Okay — let's start.

---

## Closing

Some of you may think like this: _We watch. Now what do we do?_

Nothing. Just watching your mind. ==Just watching it already makes you come back home.== That's it. ^just-watching

We have done almost six minutes today. So we have finished this week's Dhyana.

Thank you.
