## The Self Wrought Selfs Quotes

“Our entire world of experience is experienced in reference to this self-wrought self. Man, the “self-making” creature, constructs the subject of his own existence which may dwell within his self-constructed home.” ([Waldron, 2003, p. 2](zotero://select/library/items/UY8KXLFT)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9JCMV4V9?page=19\&annotation=WZMJ9E97))

“In this sense, the emergence of a “self” from the stream of inchoate experience into a relatively stable locus of self-reference and self-awareness, with all its regular and regulated cognitive and affective processes, is one of the most remarkable achievements of biological evolution and constitutes perhaps the most fundamental human technology.” ([Waldron, 2003, p. 2](zotero://select/library/items/UY8KXLFT)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9JCMV4V9?page=19\&annotation=CQQNIDCM))

## Parfit and the Buddha: Identity and Identification in Reasons and  Persons

“Whether or not it is the case that the ‘me’ who gets up tomorrow will in fact be me, today, typing this, it is certainly the case that I want to wake up tomorrow. I imagine myself waking up tomorrow, and want it to be me. I also want a number of other things to continue in an identical fashion which I identify with mysParfit and the Buddha: Identity and Identification in Reasons and  Personself: I want my body to be intact and my possessions to be still functional and not stolen, and I want my mind and reputation to be unimpaired. All of these continuities are thus functions of the ego. According to Buddhist teachings, however, this identification is not inevitable and can be lessened and ultimately removed, by spiritual practices.” ([Ellis, 2000, p. 2](zotero://select/library/items/79S9NXZY)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/DM27V2MQ?page=2\&annotation=YT4ZTXMP))
