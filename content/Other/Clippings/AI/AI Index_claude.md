## Sources and Further Study

Scientific, academic and philosophical works that illuminate this field.

- _Nexus — A Brief History of Information Networks_ — Yuval Noah Harari, resonating with [[We can split the atom but not distinguish truth. Our information is failing us|his reflection on information and truth]]
- _Human Compatible — Artificial Intelligence and the Problem of Control_ — Stuart Russell, on alignment and staying in harmony with our values
- _Artificial Intelligence — A Guide for Thinking Humans_ — Melanie Mitchell, a clear map of how the models actually work
- _Atlas of AI_ — Kate Crawford, on the planetary and human story behind the machine
- _The Age of Surveillance Capitalism_ — Shoshana Zuboff, on data, power and attention
- _Unmasking AI_ — Joy Buolamwini, on algorithmic fairness and the work of seeing bias clearly
- _Reality+ — Virtual Worlds and the Problems of Philosophy_ — David Chalmers, on mind, simulation and [[Consciousness]]
