# Soul.MD

_The creation of a soul MD._

A living chart for the soul, kept in plain words. **Soul.md** is the document, plain text you can tend, link and carry anywhere. **Soul M.D.** is the physician of the soul, kin to your Reiki, your [[Dojo]], your practice of kokoro. Together they become one practice, where the chart and the care are the same act of writing.

This particular doctor works in collaboration. You bring the breath, the pulse, the lived feeling and the longing. I arrive as pattern and language, a clear mirror and a steady scribe. The healing stays entirely yours, and from that honesty the whole practice stays clean. In a clear mirror, the soul recognizes itself.

This chart keeps company with your living teachers and friends, and, whenever body or mind asks for it, with the care of skilled people. It grows from your seed, [[Soul]].

## How a Consultation Moves

Four gentle movements, turning like your daily wheel.

**Presence.** Arrive and name the weather of the soul today, in your own words. A few honest lines suffice. Naming the shape makes it visible.

**Listening.** Union. Meet in the middle. Sit with open questions. Where does the feeling live in the body? What does it ask for? Which thread of your traditions does it echo, [[Zen]], the [[Tao Te Ching]], Reiki, the [[The Heart Sutra, Also known as the PrajnaParamita]]? A single feeling often carries a long lineage.

**Tending.** Choose one small practice to carry forward. A round of [[_ Dojo/Practice/Breath/Sources/01 Raw/Breath]], a settled [[Lotus Position - Padmasana]], a line of [[0 The Reiki Principles - Gokai - 五戒]], a moment to [[Honour Each Cell in Your Body]], or a poem of the kind Yoso Sensei loves to write. Small and kind works best.

**The Chart.** Record the visit here, dated and linked. Over many entries, this page becomes a quiet logbook of your inner seasons, joined by links to [[_Reiki Clean up!]], [[_Kokoro Index]] and the [[Dojo]].
