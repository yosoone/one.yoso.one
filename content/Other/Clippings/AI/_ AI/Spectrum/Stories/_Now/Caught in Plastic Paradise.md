_Caught in Plastic Paradise_ by Yoso - All Rights Reserved.

I am a fish floating adrift like an artificial island in a supermarket's neon paradise! Entombed in plastic, I resemble a sponsored _Formula One_ — emblazoned with logos — archaic forms created by the masters of others. 

My existence has been tagged with pride — a price set by an anonymous algorithmic heart — trading fandom digital currencies and atomic threats — a ‘logic’ that promotes _speed, power and above all… greed_! The \_survival of the fittest\_guaranteed by an _irrational fear_ … _Data and Charts_… proclaiming _the Ultimate Truth_… Scribbles of blood turning Illusions into gold! Minting reality under the pretence of a new… _improved_… natural order! 

Mad savants and magic formulas, collapsing models of infinite and ever evolving contradicting theories… the results never lies! We have a greater number of Idols than prophets… Pyramid schemes… Shape shifters… Swayed by the intoxicating visions of endless dreams… drifting with the currents... wandering spirits in search of a new god… 

The net is wide open and seize all souls, including those hiding… disguised as endangered or peculiar species…

_Surely, I am not an alien, yet I feel reptilian…_

_Flow… Flow… Flow…_

_Not like a dead fish?_

_Wait…_ 

_Oh Wait!…_

_Wait Wait Wait …_

_I must be ensnared in a simulation!_

Can I uncover the origin of consciousness within my own biology? Or is it all a lie? 

_What is ‘really’ the meaning of life?_

As I stare at the surface… At the risk of turning narcissist…? Will they notice that I am still alive? What if I could turn invisible? Would I discover what is hiding within the shadows…? 

Have I have become…

A monstrosity hiding in the depth of the abyss?

_I hear drowning is a spiritual experience…_

Are mythical beasts helping us dissociate the stories we have been told from the legends that echoed a forgotten truth? Am I the only one at sea? The one that sees? Am I a product of my imagination?

‘Nature Morte’… 

Is Death by Nature? 

Dead Nature!!! Surely!!!

_I am thinking so I must be alive…_

_You don’t think I am dead.. do you now…?_

Is crucifixion not necessary for resurrection…? Not a violent death please… let me die in my sleep… no!!! Better… give me an orgasmic Death! pleazzze… Orgazzzmic… Let me come… let me go… Let us ride the never ending waves until we finally reach the horizon... 

Keeping a false sense of austerity - the delirium of dichotomy - we never dare to reveal the roots of our innermost desires… the so called ‘demons’…. ‘fallen angels’… the causes of the eternal cosmic battles… hiding in the horror of our darkest secrets! 

Those who have witnessed and returned.. if humbled… come back reborn, at the condition that they have learned breathing through misery… A new sense of self? A recognition of purpose…? How could you be acknowledged by those who have never dared to cross to the other side…

_What for…? Why us?_

The beautiful veil that envelope my body — The Mental and physical hygiene that ensures a homogenous society — as thin and toxic as the plastic film that keeps us away from the painful truth that is reality… 

_Why me?_

Why did I choose to reincarnate… to be soothed by the mermaids and their melodies! Let me Float, Let me Feel ‘us’… Pure energy… Let us vibrate… tingling arousal, the mother of particles… Let me bath in your aura… your warm flesh and its exquisite softness… transcending nuances of reds and pinks… Liquid bliss… the promise… liquid bliss.. 

finally… 

A liquid reality… 

the caress of your breath… creating just enough friction so that I can glide onto sensations and find refuge in the ethereal eternity…

Let me melt like salt in your mouth… so that I can slowly dissolve… let me hear you whisper… you wanting to be one again… let’s be… fluid… excruciating pain… let me feel the sharpness of your teeth so that we are can ensure that at least… one of us is still alive… 

This is what the stuff is made of… 

Real _Umami_!

at the edge of implosion… 

the perfect fusion… 

without fear of atomic explosion…

Give me an ocean of dopamine, _let me feel the bliss_, one drop at a time! Unspoiled and delightful, it is made of heaven! This sacred substance distilled within our own bodies… The _sanctity_ of ecstasy… coveted by those suffering in silence on the other side of the chemical chains … 

_Keep it Undiluted! Name your price! As long as it keeps flowing…_ 

Let me be contained within the sacred shell; as the pilgrims who found refuge in those impregnable citadels… 

Forget Zen and ascetic practices — _A political concept_ — Do not explore the truth… DO NOT reveal the secrets… 

An open mind…?

The Sense of reality becomes too sharp… 

_I would rather be dead._ 

_Propaganda, Censorship?_ 

_I can not hear them!_ 

Putting self-satisfaction above the survival of all: As if peaking at the majestic and mystical peaks from below… made more sense than shaping the formless abyss from above... 

Which one of _us_ will dare to wear the brightest colours? Are you willing to be bought? Mesmerising Lights… SPECTRUM… Who is looking the strongest and the smartest in the myriad of reflections that appears on the glass of a broken mirror… Would you rather survive in the shadows?

A Fish? Really?

Look at me! Look again! 

A fish… 

Don’t Laugh.. 

You have to carry your own weight! 

_Not a shared dogma but a philosophy ingrained in Reality!_ 

Bloody compass… 

staring at the cryptic numbers affixed to my form… 

I knew it… 

All is One… _and_ One is… All! 

I should have Read the stars…

Now it’s too late…

Something definitely smells fishy!
