Yoso.One is an independent publication curated by [Yoso](https://one.yoso.one/?ref=yoso-one.ghost.io). \
Inspired by spirituality, technology and visual arts – a creative introspection, a meditation practice, an investigation into culture and artificial intelligence. Reacting to the present, rediscovering the past... Symbols for an uncertain future. 

What are the challenges? What can we leave behind? Who and what are we? What is consciousness? Do we need our bodies to experience? Should we still dream of universality?

If this is your first time, I invite you to join me on this visual journey by [subscribing](https://yoso-one.ghost.io/about/#/portal/signup) for free. If you wish to show your support, you can [become a patron](https://yoso-one.ghost.io/about/#/portal/signup), which gives you unrestricted access to the publication.

Serious Collectors, if you desire a print of what you have seen, or if you have any special requests, please  [Contact Yoso](mailto:yosoone@proton.me) directly. 

To those of you who have supported my work over the years, I am eternally grateful, you have allowed me the freedom to pursue the creative journey independently, meet awesome creative souls and embrace humanity, acquire new skills and access a previously unknown esoteric world.

It is with humility that I thank you from the depth of my heart!

May the force be with you!

**Yoso**\
Miyazaki, Japan
