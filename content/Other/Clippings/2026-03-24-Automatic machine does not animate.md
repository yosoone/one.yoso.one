## Automatic machine / no anima

Source: Clipping from [Marconoris](https://lab.marconoris.com/it/macchina-automatica-no-anima/)

![Barcelona, January 12, 2025](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/40175335100954f2ff0efebda7dcce65/Libellus/images/photos/Sin%20techo%20en%20Barcelona%20-%2012.01.2025.jpg)

Barcelona, January 12, 2025

Barcelona, January 12, 2025\
I apologize for the possible lack of respect towards the person ritratte. I have not found any other way to support the sense of this article without publishing the image. If someone saw if you recognize and wish to pick it up, you can contact me.

A man from Asian traits sleeps sitting on the threshold of a window. His expression is tense, suffering, not clear if he is still alive. From the other side of the glass, a girl is sitting at the table of a cafeteria, with the headphones, assorta in her computer. It is not evident that it is arrived, but it is clear that the presence of the man does not seem to capture his attention at all.

I remember when I arrived in Barcelona more than twenty years ago, with which surprise I looked at the indifference of the passers-by towards the condition of the homeless. I who arrived from the rich province of northern Italy, I was not used to this. It was rare to find people lying down per terra, something that was still considered a serious question. Twenty years after, the number of homeless people has increased considerably and with it, the poignant pain that causes me the systemic indifference towards this discarded and abandoned humanity. An indifferent to which my son is adapted, feeding it, as in the case of the photo that opens this line. Like all of them, I'm so satisfied with this scene that my interest towards the conditions of this man is limited to this photograph and to this one. Fosse vivo o morto, sofferte o addormentato, era questione di _altri_.

This image sows a scene of the new contemporary _Zeitgeist_. Separate from a thin glass, give a side the technological convenience of the _integrated humanity_, from other glialtri, the _scarti_. The fact in se is not new, what makes the difference is that which is not seen. Or that if it is discounted.

The questions that arise from me are diverse, but the most urgent is: who should be the _other_ person in charge of solving the emergency of a human life?

The girl with the headphones in the cafeteria we are not in front of the suffering of others. The window glass is the screen [^2] — surface that protects and projects, barrier that transforms the emergency in spectacle and the innocent viewer. The distance between a man who sleeps on a threshold and genocide that is transmitted directly is only a matter of scale. The neutral, alla luce dell'ingiustizia, sta dalla parte dell'oppressore,[^3] but the wait is also. Waiting for someone else to take care of is already a form of complicity.

L'indifference to the homeless is not individual cruelty, it's systemic automatism.\[[^4] Thus absorbed in our cycle — produce/consume/have fun/die —, we seem to be machines that have stopped registering others as humans. _Super communicated, super connected, zero emotions, zero consciousness.\[_[^5] The device is transformed into an instrument of our own submission,[^6] and with it the ability to see the other is reduced to the gesture of photographing it, commenting it and going ahead.\[[^7]
