# Dojo Index

A living map of the dojo. Here heart, body and mind unite in kokoro (心). Treat this page as your gateway. Follow any thread that calls to you, practice with kindness and confidence, and return whenever you wish to find your centre again.

> The dojo lives everywhere, beyond any single wall, present in each place you find yourself. Cultivate inner peace, make it a common practice and an art form, connect with the universal energy that binds us all, and extend your practice to those and everything around you.
> — after [[Dojo]]
