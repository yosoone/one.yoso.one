## Transcript

---

Hands joined at the heart.
Look, feel within.
Welcome home.

If you practice the art of cultivation for the good of all sentient beings, the starting point is now — it is always now.

Unite heart, body, and mind as one simply by being, breathing.
Acceptance of all your sensations, do not hide them.
Do not run away — acceptance of those sensations.

Welcome home.

---

Now, for a few moments — imagine you enter a huge room, a palace, a dojo, a resting place. Imagine that place is within you.
Welcome home.

This is your refuge.

Have a rest, perceive, breathe quietly.
Gradually feel your body correct itself.
Do not hurt yourself with rigidity — accept the flow of life.
It is not rigidity we are seeking.
It is stillness — a quiet mind.

A quiet mind amongst billions of ripples, repairs, a few momentous souls, clutches, news — still, you are home. And those sounds like raindrops in the distance.

Rinpoche says the mind is also like a lotus raising in the mud. In this vast sea of information, emotions, conflicts, paradoxes, idols — your mind can be a resting place. A resting place for yourself, a resting place for others. A well of knowledge and peace.

---

Simply become one with your breath.
Be one with your breath.

Today all my energy is dedicated to the good of all sentient beings, into harmony.

Welcome home.

---

It is the love for Yuko that brings tears to my eyes. The love of a precious one that you know you will lose one day — you know it will stop, or you think you know.

It will stop. We will die anyway — or do we?

Can we simply find refuge in the sensation of attendant love, care and compassion?

---

I notice that the candles will be done in a few minutes and will simply look at them — and not worry about the last moment.

I will celebrate daylight.
I do celebrate the [[Light]] within each one of you, and the light we bring together — as two candles bring more light than one, yet it is only light.

---

Simply rest.

Prepare yourself for the day. Precious moment, precious presence.

Note the [[Other/Nodes/Dragon]] flow — as a wave navigating freely between the two poles. Pure expression, strength, and equilibrium. Elemental.
