Freedom (Edited)

I remember the days,
First finishing high school.
Smell of summer,
tingling sensations.
Air in the air.

A promise of freedom.
What does it taste like?

I had far too much freedom for sure. Yet, I am glad to have had all those experiences.
Not counting all the friends who died or got arrested.

I had the grace of pursuing my studies in the UK, again following love and adventure.

A spontaneous journey (unplanned) as all main events always seems to have occurred in my life.

A taste of freedom, a new culture, a new language, new sounds and sensations. Love and adventure.
Thank you Megan.

Rediscovering the self, creating a new one based on study, discipline and training.
The form takes his own shape.

It makes me realise looking back that I always had a focus, except maybe during the teenage years which were much more difficult.

Then again, end of the BA. A promise and taste of freedom, wishing to connect to the mainstream and doing things properly.

I realise, I was ready to engage with this freedom, alone, freelance and part time, the path was ahead, a lot of studies and dedication that would prepare me to climb the ladder in London.

A new adventure started.
Would there be such thing as too much freedom?
