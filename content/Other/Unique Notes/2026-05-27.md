## First Thoughts

Feeling Sad! The trigger was last night looking at Yuko's new shoes! It looks like they didn't fit even tho they seem good quality. Just thinking about it again this morning brings tears to my eyes. I do love her so much, I told her shoes should fit her and not the other way around! A metaphor for life. And is shopping in a rush at the end of a long day such a good idea? She apologised for shopping, I told her don't worry! I just hope that it is good shopping and that your toes are happy!

My first thoughts of the morning were about Otosan who tries to make conversation. The last couple of years have been tough! Probably the last 6 actually! Since Okasan passed away (bones ceremony)! It makes me cry everytime the topic is brought to my eyes! Could I ever live without Yuko? Would that be the last challenge to surpass suffering, maybe to the point of not wanting to be living?

A few days ago, when I remembered the conversation Otosan was trying to make! A small touch on my tooth!

It does make me be thankful for science! I have had such a 'rough' life early I guess that most of the damage was done prior to my 25th Birthday!

I woke up, hands joined at the heart! Being grateful! My vow to honour life and spirit reinforced!

Let's trust this journey and let's give it our best!

🙏

Are those tears another liver release?
