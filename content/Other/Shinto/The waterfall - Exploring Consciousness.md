An image that came to my mind while exploring consciousness.

If the state of our [[Consciousness]] is subjective,
How does one defines consciousness, how does it impact our own life's and those we touch.

The image of the [[sacred]] waterfall is one of abundance, it can be used in [[Meditation]] similar to visualising the light from the depth of our hearts!

One can see therselves bathing in the beautiful energy that resides in nature. Or should I say pure consciousness!

The image that initially came to my mind was seeing the waterfall keep me calm, humorous, warm and smiling towards others in all situations.

It is when one find inner peace, wisdom and redefines the core of its conscious actions and how we wish to be in the world!

How would be our lives if we manifested kindness at each step and with each breath!

Are you conscious now?

How often have you realized ruminating with your mind, past events or peoples reactions, how many of those could have been avoided? How many of those situations have you created? Even considering the events that followed your reaction? Even if that situation was not comfortable or just? Was your reaction justified? If yes? Could you have reacted in a more positive attitude?
