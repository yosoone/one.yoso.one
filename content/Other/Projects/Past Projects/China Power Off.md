# China Power Off

![China Power Off](https://yopunk833100184.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/opium_war.jpg)

![China Power Off — Battersea](https://yopunk833100184.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/battersea.jpg)

A collaboration with Taiwanese artist Cao Fei & The Serpentine Gallery, London.

For the exhibition China Power Off, the project consisted of live video projections exploring Colonisation and the Opium War — questioning the colonial relationship between the British Empire and China, remixed with contemporary culture.

---

**Collaboration:** Cao Fei (caofei.com) & The Serpentine Gallery, London
**Medium:** Live Video Projection / Installation
**Theme:** Colonialism, The Opium War, Contemporary Culture
