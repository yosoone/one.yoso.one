# The Lure of Stuff

![The Lure of Stuff](https://yopunk833100184.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/ticker2.jpg)

Consumerism. War. Corporate Greed. The Stock Exchange.
How are they linked to our own needs — the needs created by others?
The abyss within us. Filling the gap. The increasing gap.

A collaboration with Victoria Whatmore, based on her original poem.

---

**Collaboration:** Victoria Whatmore
**Medium:** Installation / Audio Visual
**Based on:** Poem — "The Lure of Stuff" by Victoria Whatmore

---

_We're told what to eat_
_Told what to wear_
_The lure of stuff_
_Is always there._

_The lure of stuff_
_Is nothing but greed_
_The adman has told us_
_What we need._
