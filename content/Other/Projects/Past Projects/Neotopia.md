# Neotopia

![Neotopia](https://yopunk833100184.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/neotopia-1.png)

Interactive Prototype in collaboration with Manuela Pfrunder, based on her original Graphic Design Thesis.

Neotopia asks how the world would look if every human being had the same opportunities. It presents an imaginary new world order — a vision in which everything is redistributed on the basis of a radical approach to justice, where every human being has equal rights and can lay equal claim to all resources.

Each person receives their own country, measuring 291.5 m × 291.5 m, including an island, water, cultivated and pasture land, desert, a share of rice, car and soap production — and the liberty to handle their resources as they please.

What will each individual then own? How much island? How much ice? How long will we live in luxury? How long will we starve?
