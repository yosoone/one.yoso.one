# We Want Your Soul

![We Want Your Soul](https://yopunk833100184.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/wwys.jpg)

Initially displayed as an Installation at "X-Care" International Design Exhibition in Taipei, circa 2005.

We Want Your Soul explores the evolution of culture since the Second World War and the creation of widespread communications and propaganda — the US Machine, a mix of advertising, psychology, and the creation of Hollywood as a military device; a few of the pillars that helped shape our interpretation of consumerism as we experience it today.

In essence, a new weapon has been created — part of a much bigger Arsenal. Driven by greed, the war on freedom that protects first world interests across the globe. It is often used to justify Violence, expanding new Horizons and imposing new ideologies beyond political borders.

Post Colonial, United under a new flag — it is our duty to police the world.
