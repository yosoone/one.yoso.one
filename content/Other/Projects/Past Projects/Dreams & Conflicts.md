# Dreams & Conflicts

![Dreams & Conflicts](https://yopunk833100184.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/pigs-bckgnd-1.gif)

The Circus that passes through the town — Colourful Lights, Clowns, Rainbows and Fanfares, inviting us to Join the show.

Pretty Visuals. Mesmerise me, take me there.

Initially first shown at the OFFF Festival circa 2006 in Valencia as an Installation and Audio Visual Performance — to the general public and an international audience of designers and artists.

Dreams & Conflicts is part of a much bigger body of work exploring Culture, Power, War & the power of subliminal messages. A reflection on the power of communication and advertising in an age of Monoculture — the beautiful colours and packaging that often hide the underlying production mechanisms of a much darker industry.

Once the pretty visuals captivated the audience's attention, Dreams & Conflicts would reveal, without warning, what was hiding behind the curtain.
