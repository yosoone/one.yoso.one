# Japanese Concepts — Index

_A map of doorways, grouped by theme. Notes that exist are linked; the rest are ground still to explore. See also the fuller [[Japanese Concepts Glossary]]._

---

## The Self, Mind, and Heart

- [[Other/Nodes/Kokoro|Kokoro]] 心 — heart-mind-spirit as one undivided field
- **Mushin** 無心 — "no-mind"; clear, unclenched presence
- **Mu** 無 — nothingness, the fertile void
- **Kū** 空 — emptiness (_śūnyatā_); nothing has a fixed, separate essence
- **Honne / Tatemae** 本音・建前 — true inner feeling vs. the public face
- **Kuuki wo yomu** 空気を読む — "reading the air"

## Impermanence and Time

- **Mujō** 無常 — impermanence; all things arise, change, and pass
- [[Mono no Aware]] 物の哀れ — the gentle ache of things passing
- **Ichigo ichie** 一期一会 — "one time, one meeting"; each encounter unrepeatable
- **Ma** 間 — the charged interval; the space between
- **Yohaku no bi** 余白の美 — the beauty of blank space

## Aesthetics and Art

- **Wabi-sabi** 侘寂 — beauty in imperfection and age
- **Yūgen** 幽玄 — profound, mysterious beauty beyond words
- **Shibui** 渋い — understated elegance that deepens with time
- **Iki** 粋 — refined, effortless chic
- **Kanso** 簡素 — simplicity, the elimination of clutter
- **Datsuzoku** 脱俗 — freedom from convention
- **Kintsugi** 金継ぎ — mending with gold; honouring the break

## Practice, Discipline, and Path

- **Dō** 道 — "the way"; practice as a path of self-cultivation
- **Kata** 型 — the rehearsed form practised until free
- **Shuhari** 守破離 — keep the form, break the form, leave the form
- **Kaizen** 改善 — continuous small improvement
- **Shokunin** 職人 — the craftsman's moral vocation
- **Gaman** 我慢 — dignified endurance
- **Seijaku** 静寂 — tranquillity, active stillness

## The Sacred and the Natural

- [[Kami|Kami]] 神 — the sacred presence in mountains, rivers, trees
- **Kotodama** 言霊 — "word-spirit"; words carry spiritual power
- **Makoto** 真 / 誠 — sincerity, truthfulness of heart
- **Musubi** 結び — the generative force of binding and connection
- **Yamato-damashii** 大和魂 — the "Japanese spirit" (handle critically)
- [[Zen]] 禅 — the school of direct seeing and meditation
- **Satori** 悟り — sudden awakening
- **Kenshō** 見性 — an initial glimpse of one's true nature
- [[Gassho, Bowing and Intention|Gassho]] 合掌 — hands in prayer; bowing as presence, gratitude, and acknowledgement of life in the other

## Connection, Society, and Care

- **Wa** 和 — harmony; the _kokoro_ of Japan itself
- **Omoiyari** 思いやり — empathy that anticipates another's needs
- **Omotenashi** おもてなし — wholehearted hospitality
- **Aidagara** 間柄 — "betweenness"; the self in the relational space (Watsuji)
- **Amae** 甘え — the sweet dependence of trusting another's goodwill
- **En / Goen** 縁 — the karmic thread of connection
- **Kizuna** 絆 — the deep bond that ties people together

## Energy, Body, and Vitality

- [[ki]] 気 — life-energy, breath, vital force
- **Hara** 腹 — the belly as centre of gravity and intuition
- **Seishin** 精神 — spirit, mind, disciplined will
- **Tanden** 丹田 — the energetic centre below the navel

## Living Well

- [[Ikigai|Ikigai]] 生き甲斐 — "reason for being"
- **Kansha** 感謝 — gratitude as a practice → see also [[Gratitude]]
- **Shikata ga nai** 仕方がない — "it cannot be helped"; acceptance
- **Yutori** ゆとり — spaciousness; room to breathe
- **Mottainai** もったいない — reverence against waste
- **Komorebi** 木漏れ日 — sunlight filtering through leaves
