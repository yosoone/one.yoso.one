# A Glossary of Japanese Concepts to Explore

_A map of doorways. Some you already walk through — [[Other/Nodes/Kokoro]], [[Ikigai]], mono no aware, wabi-sabi — and those are marked. Most are new ground, chosen because they touch what you already care about: consciousness, practice, art, the sacred, and the flow of a liquid reality._

---

## The Self, Mind, and Heart

**Kokoro** 心 — heart-mind-spirit as one undivided field. The ground of your whole inquiry. → [[Other/Nodes/Kokoro]]

**Mushin** 無心 — "no-mind"; the clear, unclenched presence of the master, fully in the moment. The open hand. → already in your [[Kokoro for a Liquid Reality]]

**Mu** 無 — nothingness, emptiness, the pregnant void. Less an absence than a fertile ground from which things arise. Nishida's _zettai mu_ (absolute nothingness) builds on it.

**Kuu / Kū** 空 — emptiness in the Buddhist sense (Sanskrit _śūnyatā_); the insight that nothing has a fixed, separate essence. The philosophical root beneath _mujō_.

**Honne and Tatemae** 本音・建前 — one's true inner feeling versus the face shown in public. A whole map of how the inner and social selves negotiate.

**Kuuki wo yomu** 空気を読む — "reading the air"; sensing the unspoken mood of a room. The social form of _kokoro_'s attunement.

---

## Impermanence and Time

**Mujō** 無常 — impermanence; all things arise, change, and pass. The deep current under Japanese aesthetics. → woven through [[Kokoro for a Liquid Reality]]

**Mono no aware** 物の哀れ — the gentle ache of things passing; beauty made poignant by transience. The cherry blossom feeling.

**Ichigo ichie** 一期一会 — "one time, one meeting"; each encounter unrepeatable, therefore to be met fully. The tea ceremony's heart, and a direct teaching for a liquid reality.

**Ma** 間 — the charged interval; the silence between notes, the space between things. Emptiness as an active presence, not a gap.

**Yohaku no bi** 余白の美 — "the beauty of blank space"; the eloquence of what is left empty in ink painting and design. _Ma_ made visible on the page.

---

## Aesthetics and Art

**Wabi-sabi** 侘寂 — beauty in imperfection, simplicity, and the patina of age. The cracked bowl, the weathered beam.

**Yūgen** 幽玄 — profound, mysterious beauty that points beyond words; the felt sense of being part of a vast, dim universe. Central to Noh. → touches your [[_Shinsei]] work

**Shibui** 渋い — understated, restrained elegance; beauty that reveals itself slowly and deepens with time.

**Iki** 粋 — refined, effortless chic; an urban aesthetic of restraint and quiet sophistication. The philosopher Kuki Shūzō wrote a whole treatise on it.

**Kanso** 簡素 — simplicity, the elimination of clutter; one of the seven traditional principles of Zen aesthetics.

**Datsuzoku** 脱俗 — freedom from convention and routine; the surprise that breaks habit and opens space. Another of the Zen aesthetic principles, worth pairing with your studio practice.

**Kintsugi** 金継ぎ — mending broken pottery with gold, honouring the break rather than hiding it. A practice and a philosophy of repair.

---

## Practice, Discipline, and Path

**Dō** 道 — "the way"; any practice (tea, calligraphy, martial, ink) followed as a path of self-cultivation. The suffix in _kadō_, _shodō_, _bushidō_. → [[Dojo]] · [[Practice]]

**Kata** 型 — the form, the rehearsed pattern practised until it becomes free. Mastery through repetition until the form moves on its own.

**Shuhari** 守破離 — the three stages of mastery: _keep_ the form, _break_ the form, _leave_ the form. A complete model of how a student becomes a master.

**Kaizen** 改善 — continuous small improvement; betterment as an endless, humble practice rather than a goal.

**Shokunin** 職人 — the craftsman whose work is a moral vocation, not just a skill; devotion to the craft itself. → [[craft]]

**Gaman** 我慢 — dignified endurance; bearing the hard with patience and quiet strength.

**Seijaku** 静寂 — tranquillity, active stillness; the calm at the centre of practice. The last of the Zen aesthetic principles.

---

## The Sacred and the Natural

**Kami** 神 — the sacred presence in mountains, rivers, trees, and clean acts; the Shinto sense of the holy as immanent. → [[Kami]]

**Kotodama** 言霊 — "word-spirit"; the belief that words carry spiritual power and resonate with the essence of things. Language and reality entangled.

**Makoto** 真 / 誠 — sincerity, truthfulness of heart; approaching the world with an undivided _kokoro_. The Shinto and Confucian virtue.

**Musubi** 結び — the generative force of binding and connection; the sacred energy that ties things into relationship and brings them into being.

**Yamato-damashii** 大和魂 — the "Japanese spirit"; a contested, historically loaded term worth understanding critically as well as poetically.

**Satori** 悟り — sudden awakening, the flash of insight in Zen. Paired with _kenshō_ 見性, "seeing one's nature."

**Kenshō** 見性 — an initial glimpse of one's true nature; the first opening, often distinguished from deeper _satori_.

---

## Connection, Society, and Care

**Wa** 和 — harmony; the valuing of accord and balance in a group, the _kokoro_ of Japan itself. → present in your essays

**Omoiyari** 思いやり — empathy that anticipates another's needs before they speak; care as foresight.

**Omotenashi** おもてなし — wholehearted hospitality offered without expectation of return.

**Aidagara** 間柄 — "betweenness"; the philosopher Watsuji Tetsurō's idea that the self exists in the relational space between people, never in isolation. The ethical companion to Nishida's _basho_.

**Amae** 甘え — the sweet dependence of trusting another's goodwill; a foundational concept in Japanese psychology (Doi Takeo).

**En / Goen** 縁 — the karmic thread of connection between people; the fateful affinity that brings beings together. → relates to your [[soul mates]]

**Kizuna** 絆 — the deep bond that ties people together; connection as something that holds and structures a life.

---

## Energy, Body, and Vitality

**Ki** 気 — life-energy, breath, vital force; the animating current of body and world. → [[ki]] · [[Energy]]

**Hara** 腹 — the belly as the body's centre of gravity and intuition; the seat of courage and grounded presence. _Haragei_, "belly art," is acting from this centre.

**Seishin** 精神 — spirit, mind, will; the disciplined inner force cultivated through practice.

**Tanden** 丹田 — the energetic centre below the navel; the focal point of breath and _ki_ in meditation and martial arts.

---

## Living Well

**Ikigai** 生き甲斐 — "reason for being"; the quiet worth that makes a life feel worth waking to. → [[Ikigai]]

**Kuyō / Kansha** 感謝 — gratitude as a practice and a stance toward the world. → [[Gratitude]] · [[kokoro in gratitude]]

**Shikata ga nai** 仕方がない — "it cannot be helped"; acceptance of what lies beyond control. A double-edged phrase — wisdom and resignation both.

**Yutori** ゆとり — spaciousness; room to breathe in time, mind, and life. The opposite of the rushed and the cramped.

**Mottainai** もったいない — a pang of regret at waste; reverence for the value of things and their right use.

**Komorebi** 木漏れ日 — sunlight filtering through leaves. A small word for a fleeting beauty — _mujō_ you can stand inside on a morning walk.

---

_Where to begin: the ones most alive to your current thinking are **ichigo ichie**, **aidagara**, **ma**, **mu**, **shuhari**, and **kotodama** — each opens directly onto consciousness, liquidity, practice, or the sacred. Pull any thread and it connects to the rest._
