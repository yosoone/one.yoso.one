Webster
-------

essence
[noun](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/noun)

es·​sence [ˈe-sᵊn(t)s](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/essence?pronunciation\&lang=en_us\&dir=e\&file=essenc01 "How to pronounce essence (audio)") 

[Synonyms of _essence_](https://www.merriam-webster.com/thesaurus/essence)

1

a

**:** the permanent as contrasted with the [accidental](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/accidental#h1) element of being

b

**:** the individual, real, or ultimate nature of a thing especially as opposed to its [existence](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/existence)

a painting that captures the essence of the land

c

**:** the properties or [attributes](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/attribute#h1) by means of which something can be placed in its proper class or identified as being what it is

2

**:** the most significant element, quality, or aspect of a thing or person

the essence of the issue

3

**:** one that possesses or exhibits a quality in abundance as if in concentrated form

she was the essence of punctuality

4

a(1)

**:** a constituent or [derivative](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/derivative#h1) possessing the special qualities (as of a plant or drug) in concentrated form

also **:** a preparation of such an essence or a synthetic substitute

(2)

**:** a [volatile](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/volatile#h1) substance or [constituent](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/constituent#h1) (as of perfume)

b

**:** [ODOR](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/odor), [PERFUME](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/perfume)

5

**:** something that exists **:** [ENTITY](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/entity)
