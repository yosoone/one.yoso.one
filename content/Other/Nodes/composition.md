# Ink use

Techniques of the Ink Path
“The Way of the Ink” also refers to specific technical practices cultivated over centuries

- **Light ink (淡墨)** conveys delicacy and atmosphere, using diluted washes to suggest mist or breath.
- **Dark ink (濃墨)** expresses strength and vitality.
- **Dry ink (乾墨)**, applied with minimal water, creates crisp, textured marks suggestive of rock or bark.
