## Kakejiku printing prices

Prices as noted in December 2024, excluding tax; check the supplier's current prices before ordering. Printing costs differ depending on whether you order **washi printing only** or **scroll production and printing together**.

## Supplier text on seasons

Saved from a supplier's site (machine-translated, lightly corrected):

> Japanese people have lived carefully with the seasons, accepting the heat and the cold and changing the scroll with each of the four seasons. In this culture and climate, the hanging scroll was close to daily life: cool pictures were hung when it was hot and warm pictures when it was cold, to welcome guests.
