# Lessons from thangka painting

# Bless your work

Bring your purest presence to it.
Do everything as if you're giving it to the person you admire the most.

# Assume a new identity

If nothing could go wrong, how would you act? Don't be your normal self. That would be only to create normal.

# Operate beyond time and space

Create as if what you are doing now will be around in 500 years.
