I am not judging or attempting to re-create Heaven or Hell. It is to each one of us to learn from our experiences. Although I do believe in the Universal force, the law is the law, and I am not talking here about conventions, but rather a force that does not judge but simply acts - consequences attract consequences - there is no escaping from the results of our actions.
