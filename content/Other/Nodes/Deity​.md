A deity  is a speculative being, whether natural, supernatural or preternatural, having superhuman powers or qualities, who may be thought of as holy, divine, or sacred, whose myths are held to be true, and who can speak to and communicate with his believers. Deities are depicted in a variety of forms, but are also frequently expressed as having human form. Some faiths and traditions consider it blasphemous to imagine or depict the deity as having any concrete form.
