# What Defines Something as Toxic?

> _“The dose makes the poison.”_
> — Paracelsus, 16th-century physician

---

## The Classical Definition

Toxicity is the degree to which a substance can damage a living organism. By Paracelsus’s logic, **everything is potentially toxic** — oxygen, water, vitamins, even sunlight. What defines toxicity is the _amount_ and _context_, not the substance alone.

---

## Key Factors That Define Toxicity

|Factor                |Description                                           |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
|**Dose**              |How much enters the body                              |
|**Route of exposure** |Ingested, inhaled, injected, or absorbed through skin |
|**Duration**          |One-time vs. chronic low-level exposure               |
|**The organism**      |What’s toxic to an insect may be harmless to a human  |
|**Individual biology**|Genetics, liver function, age, and existing conditions|

---

## How Toxicity Is Measured: LD50

**LD50** is the “lethal dose for 50% of a test population.” Lower LD50 = more toxic.

|Substance      |LD50 (mg/kg)|
|---------------|------------|
|Water          |~90,000     |
|Caffeine       |~192        |
|Nicotine       |~50         |
|Botulinum toxin|~0.0000013  |

---

## Types of Toxicity

- **Acute** — Immediate damage from a single large exposure (e.g. overdose)
- **Chronic** — Slow damage from repeated small exposures (e.g. heavy metals accumulating over years)
- **Cumulative** — Builds up in the body over time (e.g. mercury in fatty tissue)
- **Carcinogenic** — Corrupts DNA rather than killing cells directly (e.g. asbestos, benzene)
- **Neurotoxic** — Specifically targets the nervous system (e.g. lead, nicotine at high doses)
- **Teratogenic** — Harmful specifically to developing fetuses (e.g. thalidomide, alcohol)

---

## The Philosophical Edge

Toxicity is ultimately a **relationship**, not a property. A substance isn’t toxic in isolation — it’s toxic _to something, at some amount, under some conditions._ This is why:

- Chemotherapy drugs are highly toxic _and_ life-saving simultaneously
- Botox is one of the most lethal substances known, yet is injected into millions of faces safely
- Vitamins A and D are essential nutrients that become toxic in excess

> **Toxic” is less a label and more a spectrum** — defined by biology, quantity, context, and time.
