## USB CONNECTION

Transferring files using a USB cable

### **How to enable USB transfer on your reMarkable**

1. Connect your reMarkable with a USB cable to your computer.
2. Tap **Settings.**
3. Tap **Storage**.
4. Here you'll see the option **Enable USB web interface**. Select this.
5. Open a browser window on your computer and enter the address **10.11.99.1**.

**Note:** Your reMarkable must be connected with the USB cable before the USB web interface can be toggled on.

This will show you a window of the content on your device, and you can drag and drop files into this window to transfer them. You don't need to be connected to Wi-Fi or the cloud to use this functionality.
