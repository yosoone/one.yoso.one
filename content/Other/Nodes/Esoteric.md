Go beyond Duality
Beyond the limits of [[language]] or reason.

A story within a story within a story...
Dividing to Justify, Dividing to exist?

Bringing reason to the mind to reveal itself,
Its own way of functioning...
Playing and moulding it...
You see, it is reasoning.

Going beyond sensations.
Integrating knowledge, Intuition, spontaneity.

Navigating between Order and Chaos.

Realising how much reality is constructed - predefined by our own thoughts.

Do we remember? Where our thoughts come from?
What is there to observe?

Let the spontaneous unfold.
