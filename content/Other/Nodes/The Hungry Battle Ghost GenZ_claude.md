# the hungry battle ghost — pov you're the ghost

_Source: Claude, Anthropic, Claude Opus 5, conversation with author, 11 September 2026. Traditional and scholarly grounding lives in [[The Hungry Battle Ghost_claude|The Hungry Battle Ghost]]._

### kyé-né — this life

_ding_

queue pop. we're SO back.

accept accept accept. the hands smash it. love these hands. bestie hands. mine for the next 40 mins.

fountain spawn, full hp, pockets full of gold. 63 lp to promo. sixty-three. i repeat it like a mantra. i live rent free in their head.

quick lore drop: my belly is stadium-sized and my throat is needle-sized, so everything hits one drop at a time.[^1] drip. drip. it's giving starvation arc.

the armour is asura drip, cracked lacquer thrifted from a war over a tree. the fruit up top is golden and crested, gods only. the roots are drinking from MY soil.[^2] make it make sense. anyway, we climb.

walk to lane. walk faster. LOCK IN.

### milam — dream

cs cs cs cs. every coin is one drop down the needle. dragon secured. drip.

time to feed the host the vision board: them, three divisions up, main character energy, randoms in chat typing "insane player." delulu is the solulu. spoon it in. they swallow, i swallow, and the belly screams for seconds.

tea downstairs: cold. window: black. 2am hits different. time is a soggy map fr.

### samten — meditation

bush. river. ward glow. waiting.

then the host breathes. like actually breathes. deep belly breath. who taught them that

wait

they're looking at me

three whole seconds of eye contact. they clock the armour, the blade, the number on repeat. they realise i'm built out of their own attention. i'm their attention in a halloween costume. caught in 4k.

freeze. npc mode. deer in headlights.

come on enemy. come on enemy. COME ON.

enemy face-checks, the fight pops off, and the gap slams shut. back to autopilot, bestie. forget me. ty.

### chikhai — dying

grey.

GREY.

the screen goes full black-and-white filter, the hands go limp, and the death timer ticks in the middle. this grey is my kitchen. i cook here.

first arrow: the death itself. tiny. one bad step, one skillshot out of the fog. it lasts one timer.

second arrow: my specialty, made to order.[^3] jgl diff. matchmaking is rigged. the lp is dropping RIGHT NOW, do you hear it dropping? type it. TYPE IT IN ALL CHAT. yes. yesss.

earth into water, water into fire, fire into wind.[^4] (old book stuff, scroll past)

respawn. they walk out holding both arrows. i hold the second one in my teeth.

40 mins in, the nexus pops. slow white bloom, and the whole screen turns pure light. the ancient light, the clear light i've been dodging for literal lifetimes. EYES SHUT. EYES SHUT.

### chönyi — luminosity

**DEFEAT**

and here come my gods, the stat screen. deaths glowing red. damage bars stacked blue. vision score: tragic. and in the middle, wearing a crown of fire:

**−21 LP**

i bow. i bow. forehead on the floor. starving and terrified at the same time, as is tradition.

old book voice, far away: _these are the radiance of your own mind._[^5]

cope. beautiful cope. bow lower.

### sidpa — becoming

lobby. chat. honor vote. and in the bottom corner, glowing like a portal:

**PLAY AGAIN**

i'm 21 lp emptier than an hour ago and the hunger is at full flood.

hand over their hand. soft. gentle. guide the cursor. one more gets it back. one more climbs the tree. one more. just one more. ONE MORE GAME.

six lights in the lobby. white is the last win, still warm. red is the enemy's banner, burning, and i want it so bad. yellow is mine: the colour of a bowl held out.

old book again: _close the entrance to the womb._[^6]

ok and?

the cursor drifts toward—

honor.

they honor the support. the one who warded all game. the one who died twice for them.

something sweet slides down the needle.

wait. what is this flavour. why is it good. i'm literally being healed rn

### segaki

client closed. the room loads back in: cold tea, black window, host standing up stiff. a cup of water.

they pour it into a plant. same energy as the old scroll, where mourners pour water on a grave marker and my whole species lines up at the stream to drink.[^7]

i drink.

the belly softens. the armour slides off one shoulder. the blade gets heavy, heavier, and clatters to the floor.

in the window reflection is the face under the hunger: some kid on a summer night, laughing with the squad, back before any of it had a number.

achievement unlocked: touched grass.

tomorrow: _ding_. tomorrow the queue pops and i wake up mouth open.

tonight: water. just this one sip.

enough.

gg.
