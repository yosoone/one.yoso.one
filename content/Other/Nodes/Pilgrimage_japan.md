After he made this declaration, Kukai threw himself off the cliff. As he fell, Buddha Shakyamuni miraculously appeared to save him so that he could live a life of service to the Dharma. In some sources, the Bodhisattva Kannon, a female form of Avalokiteshvara, is said to have appeared instead. This episode in Kukai’s life is known as ‘Cliff Jumping’ (_Shashin-ga-take_). Today, Shusshaka-ji Temple (Temple of Shakyamuni’s Appearance) stands at the foot of the mountain where the event occurred, and is the 73rd stop on Shikoku’s 88-site pilgrimage circuit.
