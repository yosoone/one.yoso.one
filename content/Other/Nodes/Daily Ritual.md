# Evening

Have everything prepared for the next day so that you have time in the morning!

## Before you fall asleep

Clear and clean all the space and thoughts, go to bed before you are exhausted, bring your mind to your heart, be grateful for the day.

Have a shower or a bath, let it refresh, clear yourself from the day.

Be grateful for the energy you have left in you. Let's look ahead to celebrating tomorrow
