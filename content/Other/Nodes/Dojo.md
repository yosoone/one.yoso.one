## The Dojo

Welcome,

First and foremost, remember: the dojo is not confined to any single place. It is everywhere – beyond its walls, wherever you find yourself. Embrace your practice with kindness and confidence, both in solitude and among others.

Take the time to nurture your own spiritual journey. Develop
Your own sense of purpose! Be kind and compassionate towards your emotions as each reveal an inner world that tries to express itself. Rest and replenish with gratitude.

Bath in nature, Honour the sunrise, the tides and the moon.

As your energy and awareness increase, your reality and sensations will refine themselves, extend your practice to those around you.

At its heart, it is about cultivating inner peace and connecting with the universal energy that binds us all.

Hearing beyond the senses, understanding beyond reason.

It is your responsibility to manifest the world you wish for yourself and others!

Embrace the journey,
take a deep breath and jump within.
