Based on the search results, the key differences between an artist biography and an artist profile are:

1. Purpose:

   - An artist biography is a factual account of the artist's life, career, and achievements, typically written in the third person.
   - An artist profile blends the artist biography with the artist statement, providing more personal and contextual information about the artist and their work.

2. Scope:

   - An artist biography focuses on the key facts and milestones of the artist's life and career.
   - An artist profile has a broader scope, covering the artist's creative process, influences, and intentions behind their work, in addition to biographical details.

3. Tone and Perspective:

   - Biographies are often written in the third person, maintaining a more objective tone.
   - Profiles can be written in either the first or third person, allowing for a more personal and intimate tone.

4. Length:

   - Biographies are typically 1-3 paragraphs in length, concise and focused.
   - Profiles may be longer, as they incorporate both biographical and statement-like elements.

In summary, an artist biography is a more factual, concise introduction to the artist, while an artist profile provides a more comprehensive and personal representation of the artist and their work. The choice between the two depends on the specific needs and goals of the artist or the organization presenting the information.
