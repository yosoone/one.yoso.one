“Pierre Hadot’s conception of philosophy as a way of life (PWL) describes how ancient philosophers practiced and prescribed education via the category of “spiritual exercises” to enable a kind of existential transformation. Such “exercises” were not intended to be limited to the “spiritual” in the colloquial English sense of the term. Rather, they were meant to express that these philosophers aimed at transforming all dimensions of themselves, not merely the intellect. Aiming “to capture the full range of the ‘exercises’ at stake in the ancient philosophical writings... all of these exercises aimed at transforming the inner life of philosophers” (Sharpe 2023).” (Taylor and Kramer, 2024, p. 1)
