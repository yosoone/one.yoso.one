- List Patrons and Sponsors

Collectors and  patrons in the arts play different roles. Collectors primarily focus on acquiring and owning artworks. Their interest often lies in the value, aesthetics, or historical significance of the pieces. Collectors might buy art as an investment, for personal enjoyment, or to preserve cultural heritage.

Patrons, on the other hand, primarily support the creation of new art. They may fund artists, projects, or institutions, enabling the production of art that might not otherwise be financially viable. This support can be through direct financial assistance, sponsorships, or commissions. Patrons often help artists sustain their careers and contribute to the broader artistic community.

Remember to offer multiple tiers or open-ended donation amounts to accommodate different levels of patronage.
