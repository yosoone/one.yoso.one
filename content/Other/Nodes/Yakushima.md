Here’s the information I found about photography projects and resources on Yakushima:

**1. Photography Trek Through Yakushima – National Parks of Japan**\
A full‑day guided photography tour that lets you practice techniques such as slow‑motion, time‑lapse, drone work, and more while exploring Yakushima’s landscapes. The tour runs year‑round and includes detailed instruction from a professional photographer.

**2. “Untouched Wilderness: 11 Breathtaking Photogenic Spots on Yakushima Island (2024 Edition)” – tsunagu Japan**\
A roundup of the island’s most photogenic locations, including:

- Jōmon Sugi (ancient cedar, 2,000–7,200 years old)
- Shiratani Unsuikyo moss‑covered forest
- Remote mountains, valleys, beaches, waterfalls, and wildlife habitats.\
  The article notes that many spots require a hike, but there are easier options like Yakusugi Land for beginners.

**3. Stock Photo Collections**\
Shutterstock hosts thousands of Yakushima images—landscapes, waterfalls, forests, wildlife, and coastal scenes—that can be licensed for commercial or personal use.

**4. Local Photographer & Magazine Projects**

- **Rikiya Nakamura** (SAUNTER Magazine) has documented Yakushima in several projects, including collaborations with Yamatomichi.
- The **Yakushima Photography Festival** (a Franco‑Japanese cross‑cultural initiative) showcases works inspired by the island’s unique environment.

These resources should give you a solid starting point for planning a photography project on Yakushima, whether you’re looking for guided tours, inspirational locations, stock imagery, or local artistic collaborations. Let me know if you’d like more detail on any of these items!
