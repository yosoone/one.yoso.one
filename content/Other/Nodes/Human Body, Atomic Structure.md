## Atomic Structure

![[Other/Nodes/99 Files/IMG_6631.jpeg]]

The human body is composed of approximately **7 × 10²⁷ atoms** (7 billion billion billion), with the majority being hydrogen, oxygen, and carbon, which together make up about 99% of the total atoms\[2]\[3]\[6]. Specifically:

- **Hydrogen**: ~62% of all atoms (~4.7 × 10²⁷ atoms).
- **Oxygen**: ~24% (~1.8 × 10²⁷ atoms).
- **Carbon**: ~12% (~7 × 10²⁶ atoms)\[2]\[4].

The remaining 1% includes elements like nitrogen, calcium, phosphorus, and trace elements essential for life\[3]. These atoms are constantly replaced as cells regenerate and molecules are exchanged with the environment\[4].
