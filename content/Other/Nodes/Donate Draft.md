Root [[Finances]]

1. **Donation Button on Website**: Incorporate a donation button on the artist's or organization's website. This can be linked to a payment platform like PayPal, Stripe, or Square.

2. **Patreon or Membership Platforms**: Utilize platforms like Patreon, where supporters can subscribe for exclusive content or perks.

3. **Crowdfunding Campaigns**: Launch campaigns on platforms like Kickstarter or Indiegogo for specific projects, allowing patrons to contribute.

4. **Social Media Fundraising**: Use social media platforms that have built-in fundraising tools, like Facebook's donation button.

5. **Online Fundraising Events**: Host virtual events or auctions where attendees can donate.
