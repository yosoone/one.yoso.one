The Relationship between master and student is a sacred one, whether it lasts a moment or a lifetime.

The communication is the basis for the mutual agreement. It should be fair for both parties.

We should assume that the responsibility is at foremost the Teacher's Responsibility when it comes to methodology and safety whether concerning Exoteric or [[Esoteric]] teachings.

It is however important to insist on the student's responsibilities.

The student needs to find the teachings of the master appropriate to his quest for learning. Integrity and commitment are the foundations for mutual growth and understanding.

The student is free to leave at any time, and so is the master. It is foremost a mutual relationship based on respect.

Should that respect be compromised at any time, means that the relationship is not fruitful for the development of both the master and the student.
