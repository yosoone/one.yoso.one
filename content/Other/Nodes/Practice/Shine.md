**Note on Fame or Hiding**
_Based on the Vimalakirti Commentary (Q\&A)_
(Not an exact transcript)

"Fame like many others,
There is a desire to be famous, and also a desire to be not known. Both are wishes.

Both can be tools or a hideout for our egos,
Fame is an obvious hideout for the ego, as out of the ego's insecurity - the ego feels that if you are famous you will be more secure...

"Sticking with the [[Vimalakirti Sutra]] point of view,

**You should be free from fame, but you should also be courageous enough to be famous if it is beneficial to yourself and others.**

If you do not do that, you are hiding (as a disguise)
, in the zone of not known. and that may not necessarily be kind and compassionate.. ""

Ref:\_

**Vimalakirti Sutra, New Delhi, India, 16 - 17 March 2018 - Part 1**
