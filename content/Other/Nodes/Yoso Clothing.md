### A Harmonious Fusion of Tradition and Innovation

A timeless blend of Bushido spirit, artisanal craftsmanship, and 'Zen' philosophy, reimagined for modern life. From the ancient wisdom of our ancestors to the cutting-edge world of cyberpunk, our ethos remains unwavering. Our designs encourage freedom and individuality, celebrating the collective, seamlessly merging traditional aesthetics with contemporary culture.

### **A Journey Through Time**

Created for the Soul traveller.

Our clothing embodies a harmonious balance of opposites: the serene and the dynamic, the traditional and the modern. We strive to create pieces that are both comfortable and stylish, suitable for both indoor and outdoor activities.

A series of Layers, 'passe-partout', allowing the wearer to travel the streets, go to private views ; in essence transcends social circles and gender perception.

### A Commitment to Integrity and Peace

We are dedicated to promoting integrity and inner peace through our designs. By combining traditional craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetics, we aim to create wearables that nourish the soul. By aiming for universality, we encourage dialogue.

### A Collaborative Vision

We believe in the power of collaboration. By uniting skilled artisans, innovative designers, and mindful individuals, we can create truly exceptional garments. Our dream is to build a legacy of ethical and sustainable fashion, empowering ourselves and others to make a positive impact on the world.

Key Themes:

- Timelessness: Blending ancient wisdom with modern design.

- Harmony: Balancing tradition and innovation, yin and yang.

- Integrity: Upholding ethical principles and sustainable practices.

- Community: Fostering collaboration and empowering individuals.

- Mindfulness: Promoting inner peace and well-being.
