**Publishing**
Webtoon - Tapas - Website - Patreon - Line?

Pros and Cons:

I definitely think if you can, hosting your comics on different platforms to reach a larger audience is always beneficial!

I use both websites and these are my personal thoughts...

**Tapastic**\
**Pros:** User friendly\
Profile Page\
Easy to get updates and interact with your readers\
Friendly community\
**Cons:** I personally have a hard time getting my comic noticed

**Webtoons**\
**Pros:** Easy to gain readers (I double reader count on webtoons than I do on tapastic)\
Readers can rate your comic\
**Cons:** Not user friendly. I find LINE and Webtoons really confusing\
Mostly benefits the big name comics I find

That's what I've taken away from using these platforms for the past year and a half. Some of my lack of experience which resulted in cons for each site might me my failure as a creator, and others may not struggle with the same issues.\
I would also like to know other creator's and reader's opinions of the two are as well.
