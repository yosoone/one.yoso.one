### Etymology

From [Middle English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_English "w:Middle English") _[luxurie](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/luxurie#Middle_English "luxurie")_, from [Old French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_French "w:Old French") _[luxurie](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/luxurie#Old_French "luxurie")_, from [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "w:Latin") _[luxuria](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/luxuria#Latin "luxuria")_ (“rankness, luxury”), from _[luxus](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/luxus#Latin "luxus")_ (“extravagance, luxury”).

### Luxus Etymology 1

From [Proto-Italic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Italic_language "w:Proto-Italic language") _[\*luksos](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=Reconstruction:Proto-Italic/luksos\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Reconstruction:Proto-Italic/luksos (page does not exist)")_, from [Proto-Indo-European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Indo-European_language "w:Proto-Indo-European language") _\*lug-so-s_, from Proto-Indo-European _[\*lewg-](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Indo-European/lewg- "Reconstruction:Proto-Indo-European/lewg-")_ (“bend, twist”), whence also _[luctor](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/luctor#Latin "luctor")_(“wrestle”).[[3]]\(https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/luxus#cite\_note-4) Cognate with [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit "w:Sanskrit") _[रुग्ण](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A3#Sanskrit "रुग्ण")_ (rugṇá, “bent, broken”), [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek "w:Ancient Greek") _[λύγος](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%BB%CF%8D%CE%B3%CE%BF%CF%82#Ancient_Greek "λύγος")_ (lúgos, “twig, withe”), [Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language "w:Lithuanian language") _[lugnas](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/lugnas#Lithuanian "lugnas")_, [Old Norse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Norse "w:Old Norse") _[lykna](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=lykna\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "lykna (page does not exist)")_ (“to bend, close”).
