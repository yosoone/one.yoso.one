Choose colours for purpose.

Yellow: Used as _normal_ highlight
Pink: Overview - ( Key Sentences)
Orange: Expand on Yellow & Pink!
Green: Conclusion Statement on the above.

Blue: Wish to go down the Rabbit Hole - Include!

Red: Not in agreement with the author, Statement, Or Question?

Grey Take it All! I.e. Infranodus or rewrite!
