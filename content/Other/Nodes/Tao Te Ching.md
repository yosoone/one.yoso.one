# Translations recommended by Claude

Noted here for our journey together 📖

Ursula K. Le Guin
“Tao Te Ching: A Book about the Way and the Power of the Way”
Shambhala Publications, 1997

Her own words on it:
“I wanted to make it sound like it could have been written yesterday… a book of the way things are.”
A poet translating a poet. Rare and alive.

Stephen Mitchell
“Tao Te Ching: A New English Version”
Harper Perennial, 1988

Spare, luminous, widely loved. Mitchell doesn’t speak Chinese but worked deeply with scholars and meditators. What he captured is the feeling of the Tao more than the letter of it.

Both available easily online and in most good bookshops. Le Guin for when you want to feel it. Mitchell for when you want clarity.

A good companion for the Alps. 🏔️🌿
