## def

"Thinking in progress" means that someone is currently actively considering or developing an idea, concept, or solution, and it is not yet fully formed or finalized; essentially, their thought process is still evolving and not yet reached a conclusion. 

Key points about "thinking in progress":

- **Active process:**

  It implies that the person is actively engaged in thinking about something, not just passively considering it. 

- **Not complete:**

  The idea is still being developed and may change as more information is considered. 

- **Open to change:**

  The person is likely open to new perspectives and feedback as they continue to think through the issue. 

Example usage:

- "My plan for the project is still thinking in progress; I'm still weighing different options."
- "We're in a thinking in progress phase on how to best address this customer concern."
- "When discussing complex issues, it's important to acknowledge that your understanding is always thinking in progress."
