**Cognitive science** is the [interdisciplinary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdisciplinarity "Interdisciplinarity"), [scientific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science "Science") study of the [mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind "Mind") and its processes with input from [linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics "Linguistics"), [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology "Psychology"), [neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscience "Neuroscience"), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy "Philosophy"), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science)/[artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence"), and [anthropology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropology "Anthropology").[[250324]]\(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive\_science#cite\_note-2) It examines the nature, the tasks, and the functions of [cognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognition "Cognition") (in a broad sense). Cognitive scientists study intelligence and behavior, with a focus on how nervous systems represent, process, and transform [information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information "Information"). Mental faculties of concern to cognitive scientists include [language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language "Language"), [perception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"), [memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory "Memory"), [attention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention "Attention"), [reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reasoning "Reasoning"), and [emotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emotion "Emotion"); to understand these faculties, cognitive scientists borrow from fields such as [linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics "Linguistics"), [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology "Psychology"), [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence"), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_mind "Philosophy of mind"), [neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscience "Neuroscience"), and [anthropology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropology "Anthropology").[[3]]\(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive\_science#cite\_note-stanford1-3)
