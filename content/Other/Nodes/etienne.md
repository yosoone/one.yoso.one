The name 

Étienne has its roots in the ==French language, where it is the equivalent of the English name Stephen==. Its deeper origin can be traced to the ancient Greek name _Stephanos_ (Στέφανος), which means "crown" or "garland". 

The name gained prominence due to Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr, who was stoned to death in the 1st century CE. In classical Greek times, a crown or garland was a symbol of honor and victory, typically bestowed upon champions. By associating the name with the first martyr, it also came to symbolize the ultimate victory of faith. 

Over time, the Greek _Stephanos_ evolved through Latin into _Stephanus_ and then into its various forms across Europe, with Étienne becoming the standard French version during the Middle Ages. 

The name's variations in other languages include: 

- **English:** Stephen, Steven
- **Spanish:** Esteban, Estéfano
- **Italian:** Stefano
- **German:** Stefan
