Briefing 7 — Grants & Opportunities

Search for the latest grants, residencies, fellowships, and open calls relevant to this profile: artists and scholar-practitioners working in Japan or with Japan as subject, and artists working at the intersection of spirituality, sacred traditions, and contemporary practice.

Japan residencies & open calls — search:

```
•	https://air-j.info/en/ — dedicated Japan residency database, updated weekly
•	https://www.jpf.go.jp/e/ — Japan Foundation official grants
•	https://www.bunka.go.jp/english/ — Agency for Cultural Affairs Japan
•	https://bombmagazine.org/discover/fellowships-residencies/ — includes Japan-specific programs
```

Buddhist studies & sacred arts funding — search:

```
•	https://www.acls.org/programs/robert-h-n-ho-family-foundation-in-buddhist-studies/ — Ho Family Foundation via ACLS, the most serious Buddhist studies grant program globally
•	https://www.rhfamilyfoundationglobal.org/arts-culture/buddhist-art/ — Buddhist art grants for museums and scholars
•	https://khyentsefoundation.org/grants-and-scholarships/ — monitor for reopening; on hiatus as of March 2026
•	https://www.sainsbury-institute.org — Robert and Lisa Sainsbury Fellowships for Japanese arts scholars
```

General curated opportunities — search:

```
•	https://www.artworkarchive.com/call-for-entry/complete-guide-to-2026-artist-grants-opportunities — updated throughout the year
•	https://www.thisiscolossal.com — monthly open calls, residencies, and grants
```

Structure each briefing in three sections:

Immediate — closing within 30 days
Flag these as urgent. Name, eligibility, deadline, amount or duration, direct link, one sentence on relevance to an artist in Miyazaki working across sacred traditions and contemporary practice.

Coming up — closing within 3 months
Same format as above.

Ongoing or rolling — no fixed deadline
Programs worth knowing about regardless of timing.

Close with one recommendation: the single opportunity that best fits an artist or scholar-practitioner based in Miyazaki working across Japanese sacred art, haiku, and Buddhist contemplative traditions.

Rules:

```
•	Flag clearly if Khyentse Foundation grants have reopened
•	Do not include generic arts grants with no connection to Japan, spirituality, or contemplative practice
•	Tone: practical and clear — this section is for action, not reflection
•	All links inline, no separate sources section
```
