\*Celebrating each moment & feeling, the ink flows as the heart beats...\
What appears so obvious at the first glance, becomes an opportunity to grow beyond what is seen.

What is the meaning of the trace that captivates our attention?\
How does it resonate with our being?

Some believe that the line is alive..\
If that is true..

Are Humility and integrity the only ways to approach Irezumi's mysterious ways?

A myriad of shades, patterns and symbols...\
How does one become so certain?

Hidden for some... A symbol of rebirth to others,\
Only the one who fully commits, will find the true meaning of what he chose to carve...\*
