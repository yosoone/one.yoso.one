# Colophon

_This site is tended by **Yoso**, based in Miyazaki, Japan._

\*A living inquiry — into 'self', meditation, prayer, culture, the development of a personal practice, and the nature of life and experiences. This exercise is to document my research, explore new interests and aspects of my personal work. I hope this might help linking knowledge to wisdom; the creation of a curriculum and a Dojo in Japanese nature. A safe haven to refresh with an open mind and open heart.

Nodes are in various states of completeness, including clippings from the web, personal thoughts and notes, writing experiments and experiments or research with AI.

Japan, it's culture, my ever evolving understanding while seeking universal concepts will be documented amongst,  more personal notes and other branches of interests.

This notes are offered freely. Nothing here is final. Everything here is sincere.

In the spirit of intention and compassion which leads everything I do, you are welcome to get in touch if you have any questions, or if you can help clarify or guide some of my research.
