# Etymology

Borrowed from [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "w:Latin") _[integer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/integer#Latin)_ (“whole”), from [Proto-Italic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Italic_language "w:Proto-Italic language") _[\*əntagros](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Italic/%C9%99ntagros "Reconstruction:Proto-Italic/əntagros")_ (“untouched”). [Doublet](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#doublet "Appendix:Glossary") of _[entier](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/entier#English "entier")_ and _[entire](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/entire#English "entire")_. Related to [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language "w:English language") _[tact](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/tact#English "tact")_, _[thack](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/thack#English "thack")_, and _[thwack](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/thwack#English "thwack")_.

### Noun

**integritās** f (_genitive_ **[integritātis](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/integritatis#Latin "integritatis")**); _[third declension](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Latin_third_declension "Appendix:Latin third declension")_

1. [soundness](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/soundness#English "soundness")
2. [integrity](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/integrity#English "integrity"), [blamelessness](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/blamelessness#English "blamelessness")
3. (of a woman) [chastity](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/chastity#English "chastity")
4. (of language) [correctness](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/correctness#English "correctness"), [purity](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/purity#English "purity")
5. ([figuratively](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#figurative "Appendix:Glossary")) the [whole](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/whole#English "whole")
