## Cell signaling

]\(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell\_signaling)

In biology, cell signaling is the process by which a cell interacts with itself, other cells and the environment. Cell signaling is a fundamental property of all cellular life in prokaryotes and eukaryotes. Typically, the signaling process involves three components: The signal, the receptor and the effector. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_signaling)
