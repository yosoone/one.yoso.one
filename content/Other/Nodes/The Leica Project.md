# Lemo

Below is a practical outline you can turn into a slide deck (or PDF) to pitch Leica for financial and gear support. Feel free to adapt the wording, visuals, and length to match your own style and the specific Leica contact you’ll be reaching out to.

---

## 1️⃣ Title Slide

- **Project name** – e.g., _“Kami Chronicles: Sacred Spirits of Japan”_
- Your name & role (photographer, researcher, storyteller)
- Contact info (email, phone, website/portfolio)
- Date

---

## 2️⃣ Executive Summary (30‑45 seconds)

A short, punchy paragraph that answers three questions at a glance:

|What|Why|How|
|---|---|---|
|**What** – A visual documentary of kami‑related sites across Japan (temples, shrines, natural sanctuaries).|**Why** – Showcases Japan’s intangible heritage, aligns with Leica’s legacy of capturing timeless moments.|**How** – Using Leica’s flagship camera system (M10‑R, SL2‑S, lenses) plus a modest sponsorship budget for travel, permits, and production.|

---

## 3️⃣ Project Vision & Storytelling Angle

- **Core narrative:** “From mountain peaks to forest groves, kami are the unseen guardians that shape Japan’s landscape and culture.”
- **Visual tone:** Quiet reverence, natural light, intimate details—exactly the kind of imagery Leica cameras excel at rendering.
- **Outcome:** A series of high‑resolution photographs, a limited‑edition coffee‑table book, an online exhibition, and a short behind‑the‑scenes video.

---

## 4️⃣ Cultural & Market Relevance

- **Cultural depth:** Kami are central to Shinto, Buddhism, folk traditions, and contemporary art. Your project will document festivals, shrine rituals, sacred trees, and pilgrimage routes.
- **Audience reach:**
  - **Photography community** – Leica enthusiasts, fine‑art collectors.
  - **Travel & culture media** – National Geographic, Lonely Planet, NHK.
  - **Japanese heritage institutions** – Museums, cultural ministries.
- **Leica branding fit:** Leica is synonymous with craftsmanship, heritage, and storytelling—values mirrored in the kami tradition.

---

## 5️⃣ Deliverables & Timeline (12 months)

|Phase|Duration|Key Milestones|
|---|---|---|
|**Research & Permissions**|2 mo|Secure filming/photography permits at 8–10 sites (e.g., Kinkaku‑ji, Kumano Kodo, Aso Shrine).|
|**Field Shooting**|6 mo|Travel to 5 regions (Kansai, Chubu, Tōhoku, Kyushu, Hokkaido); capture 300+ curated images.|
|**Post‑Production**|2 mo|Editing, color grading, sequencing; produce 50‑image editorial spread + 10‑minute video.|
|**Publication & Promotion**|2 mo|Print book (≈500 copies), online gallery, press kit, Leica co‑branded launch event.|

_(Adjust dates to fit your schedule.)_

---

## 6️⃣ Sponsorship Request

|Item|Requested Support|Value to Leica|
|---|---|---|
|**Camera & Lens Kit** (Leica M10‑R + 35 mm f/1.4, 50 mm f/1.2, SL2‑S + 24‑70 mm)|In‑kind loan for 12 months|All images shot with Leica gear → guaranteed Leica branding on every visual asset.|
|**Financial Grant**|€15 000 (travel, accommodation, permits, printing)|Logo placement on book cover, exhibition signage, and all digital assets; exclusive Leica photographer credit.|
|**Co‑marketing**|Joint press release, social‑media amplification (target 200 k+ impressions)|Direct exposure to Leica’s premium audience and to cultural‑heritage media outlets.|

Feel free to break the numbers down further (flight costs, local guides, insurance, etc.) if you want a fully itemized budget.

---

## 7️⃣ Exposure & ROI for Leica

- **Print & Digital Credits** – Leica logo on book cover, back‑cover acknowledgment, and on the exhibition wall.
- **Social Media** – 10‑15 posts per month across Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook, each tagging @LeicaCamera and using #LeicaKamiChronicles. Expect ~30 k organic reach per post (based on typical Leica campaign metrics).
- **Press Coverage** – Pitch to photography magazines (e.g., _Digital Photo Pro_, _Leica Fotografie_), travel journals, and Japanese cultural publications. Anticipated 8–10 feature articles.
- **Event Activation** – Launch event at a Leica store or partner gallery in Tokyo/Osaka, inviting influencers, journalists, and Leica ambassadors.

---

## 8️⃣ Sample Visual Mock‑up (optional)

Insert a placeholder slide showing a mock‑up of a photo taken with a Leica camera (you can use a royalty‑free image for now) with a faint Leica watermark and a caption like “Capturing the spirit of Takayama’s sacred forest”. This demonstrates the aesthetic you’ll deliver.

---

## 9️⃣ Call‑to‑Action & Next Steps

- **Meeting request:** “I’d love to discuss the project in detail over a brief call next week.”
- **Materials ready:** Portfolio link, detailed budget spreadsheet, and draft press kit attached.
- **Contact:** Your phone, email, and a QR code linking to an online portfolio.

---

## 🔟 Appendix (if needed)

- **Portfolio highlights** – 5–6 of your strongest images (preferably with a Leica aesthetic).
- **Letters of support** – From local cultural organizations or scholars (adds credibility).
- **Legal & Insurance** – Brief note confirming you have appropriate model releases and travel insurance.

---

### Quick Tips for Polishing the Pitch

1. **Keep it visual.** Leica loves clean, high‑quality imagery. Let the photos speak louder than the text.
2. **Show alignment.** Quote Leica’s brand statements (“capturing the world with precision”) and mirror that language.
3. **Be concise.** Aim for a 10‑slide deck (≈5 minutes read) – busy executives appreciate brevity.
4. **Personalize.** Mention any prior Leica work you’ve done or a personal story connecting you to the culture.
5. **Follow‑up.** Send a thank‑you email within 48 hours, attaching the deck and a short recap of the conversation.

---

### Ready to start?

1. Draft the slide titles above in PowerPoint/Keynote/Canva.
2. Populate each slide with brief bullet points (keep text < 30 words per slide).
3. Insert high‑resolution sample photos (your own or mood‑board).
4. Export as PDF and attach to an introductory email to Leica’s partnership team (e.g., <partnerships@leica-camera.com>).

Good luck—your vision of the kami, captured through Leica’s lens, could become a compelling showcase of both cultural heritage and photographic excellence!
