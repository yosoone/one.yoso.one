In [hermeneutics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermeneutics "Hermeneutics"), the **hermeneutic circle** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language "German language"): _hermeneutischer Zirkel_) is an iterative process of understanding a text (or an element of a text) through its [context](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Context "Context"). <mark style="background:rgba(92, 92, 92, 0.2)">It expresses the idea that interpretations of a part and the whole are mutually interdependent: an understanding of the whole is reached by reference to the individual parts, but an understanding of each individual part depends on an understanding of the whole</mark>. The circle is a metaphor for the procedure of <font color="#2DC26B"><mark style="background:rgba(140, 140, 140, 0.12)">transforming</mark></font> one's understanding of the part and the whole through iterative recontextualization. ^1
