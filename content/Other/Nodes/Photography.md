I feel one of the issue with photography, when me keep chasing and instinctively shoot, is that we become hunters, stalkers, without the necessary processing time to absorb the process.

Often, I feel like, it is not right, and while it is great to document life and shoot people whet they least expect it; there is a lack of respect if we do not choose the right moment --- it turns in some kind of sensationalism when revealing an element of surprise...

In a sense, it is not of order to document life,

# Yoso and Photography

I love my GR, had a few over the years, the 35mm was my first one and outstanding!

I had the chance of getting a manual Nikon very young! This taught me all the foundations! Then, later, enjoyed the black room till I would see people playing in the sun! I had enough!

Later came digital, 1000’s of pics that inspired me, but didn’t know what to do with it. I stopped photography!

After moving to Japan, in a new culture and visual language, a friend gave me his old Gr2, I felt in love with it! It broke a few years later and I bought the GR3.

It’s almost perfect for my use case!
Always on me, not always in the hand, sometimes too late.

I use it mainly to document moment in life, and key inspirations! It’s a lifelong project! No rush, I like to get the scene and the atmosphere in the frame, but sometimes, I would like something snappier, view finder, with a 28-80 lens, with a real analog feel when switching settings fast! Without all the buttons and options I never need! I mostly shoot in aperture mode, Is Leica the answer?

As i try to keep things simpler, I am also considering just the phone, but haven’t committed to it yet. Even tho, I have to admit that The Leica app gives it a really nice feel. It’s not analogous enough for my taste. Not snappy at all!

Best wishes!

🙏

Yoso
