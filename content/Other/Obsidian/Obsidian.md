**Why I use Obsidian** a.k.a _Mycloud_

Every day, daydreaming or focused on the task in front of us, we are subjected to a flow of new and old ideas: a stream of [[thoughts]] occupies our attention: Whether directly related to the task in front of us, a task ahead, a conversation we had earlier or a dream we had the night before! - Whether we worry that we will forget newly acquired knowledge, or with to create a new think tank.

Thoughts can occur as linear, or appear as random when jumping from one thought to another. They are often the results of unplanned events or unknown processes happening in the back of our minds.

Do we control 'our' thoughts, or do thoughts control us?

Meditation helps us cultivate the attention and awareness that helps us observe the stream of consciousness - depending on how much time, contextual knowledge and perspective we have to carefully consider each thought that occupies our mind, with the hope that we might be able to categorise each thought and discard the unnecessary or toxic ones.

Thinking has a purpose, a function that helps us learn new tasks or organise a complex system of symbols - hoping that it leads us to a more efficient way of being; and for those who want more, eventually a peaceful and creative life.

As Bertrand Russel (May 18, 1872–February 2, 1970)" listed in his _Ten Commandments of Critical Thinking and Democratic Decency_, that, as a teacher, he wished to promulgate;

"Do not feel envious of the happiness of those who live in a fool’s paradise, for only a fool will think that it is happiness." - The Autobiography of Bertrand Russell: 1944-1969

Each one of us has the responsibility for our happiness,
If I envy those who enjoy the ritual of daily life with simple actions. Some of us are dealing with more complex thoughts - which ever the case, reality is a symmetry of our thinking, new ways of managing those thoughts are aways welcome!

Whether a matter of filtering the redundant or toxic thoughts, by writing them down, we are able to create and cultivate our mind like a sacred [[digital garden]] that reflects how we think, and what are the most important symbols that guide us through life.  Considering each thought carefully -  are they necessary - Can we remove the clutter as if we did the spring or new year cleaning, or prune our trees for those who do! can we help the most beautiful thoughts grow? Can those thoughts help growing stronger roots for compassion.

Two tools I Iike to use to organise my thoughts and references are [[01 CORE/TO SORT_/Obsidian]] and [[Zotero]] (more on Zotero later).

Obsidian, a note taking app allows us to document through the creation of nodes (similar to a wiki) and helps us visualise the connections between all nodes. In a sense, explore linear and non-linear thinking.
