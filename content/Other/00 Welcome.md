# The Dojo

The dojo is not a place. It is a quality of attention brought to each action. It lives in the 5am silence before the house wakes, in the hands pressed together before beginning, in the needle meeting skin, in the breath that returns after distraction. The practice is simple and unfinished. It asks only to be done.

_— Yoso, Miyazaki_

## Daily Practices

_Practices section compiled by Claude, Anthropic, claude-sonnet-4-6, conversation with author, 3 June 2026._

### Morning

- **Wake** — before the mind hardens. The liquid moment. → [[01 Morning Meditation aka align, set your intention|Morning Meditation]]
- **Meditation** — sit before the day begins. Let the mind settle like water. → [[Kokoro/_ Kokoro Index|Kokoro Index]]
- **Gassho** — hands in prayer. Acknowledge the life in everything before touching anything. → [[Gassho Index|Gassho 合掌]]
- **Green tea** — one cup, present. The first conscious act of the day.
- **Breath** — return to it always. The bridge between body and spirit. → [[_ Dojo/Practice/Breath/Sources/01 Raw/Breath]]

### Body

- **Ki** — feel the energy moving. Notice where it stagnates, where it flows. → [[ki|Ki 気]]
- **Reiki** — channel, don't generate. The hands know what the mind does not. → [[_Reiki Clean up!]]
- **Vagus nerve / nervous system** — care for the ground of sensation. Rest is practice. → [[Vagus Nerve]] · [[Nervous system]]
- **Physical practice** — movement as purification. The vessel must be clean.

### Creative

- **Tattooing** — ceremony, not service. Read the person before touching skin. → [[Tattoo Dharma|Tattoo Dharma]]
- **Shinsei** — mindful creation. The practice of making as spiritual act. → [[_Shinsei|Shinsei]]
- **Writing** — the ReMarkable as a practice of noticing. One thought at a time.
- **Ink** — the line as meditation. Slow, intentional, present. → [[Ink|Ink]]

### Contemplative

- **Inquiry** — hold the question without forcing an answer. Kokoro as method. → [[Kokoro for a Liquid Reality|Kokoro for a Liquid Reality]]
- **Study** — read slowly. One book open at a time. → [[01 Dojo Index_claude|Sources & Further Study]]
- **Silence** — the practice beneath all practices. _Ma_ — the charged interval.

## The Question That Cuts Through

_"Is this pure?"_

Not morally pure in a rigid sense. Pure as in: uncontaminated by fear, ego, performance, or distraction. Aligned with the source. If yes — move toward it. If not yet — wait and purify more.

→ [[Tattoo Dharma|Tattoo Dharma]]
