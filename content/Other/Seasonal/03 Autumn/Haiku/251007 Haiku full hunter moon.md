End of Summer Full moon,
Bright in the East…
I could feel the cold wind in the morning rise…
Summer afternoon…
Feeling the Rain coming when you set your light…
It took me half a century to recognize, that both the Sun and you…
Made me who I have become…
A modern man seeking revenge on modern civilisation..
For hiding your beauty for so long..
I am back, and choose to celebrate your dance and gentle light, until the last day of my winter…
Let me celebrate many sunrises…
