# Caught in Plastic Paradise

_A drop in the ocean, ripples for a liquid reality_

## ![[Other/Nodes/Online Files/caught-in-plastic-paradise/caught-in-plastic-paradise.jpg]]

I am a fish floating adrift like an artificial island in a supermarket's neon paradise! Entombed in plastic, I resemble a sponsored _Formula One_, emblazoned with logos; residue of archaic forms created by masters of the past. 

My existence has been tagged with pride — a currency set by an anonymous algorithmic heart — trading fandom digital currencies and atomic threats, a ‘logic’ that promotes, _speed_, _power_ and above all… _greed_\_! 

Technocracy is the new church… The pyramid schemes replacing the survival of the fittest by the optimal… a race of influencers fueled by ego and virtual _currencies_… starving for infinite power… armed with data and charts… — The algorithm needs to be fed — proclaiming the need of virtual maps to navigate a PROfound reality… profiled with the promise of priceless treasures, scribbles of blood turning illusions into… hypocrisy… minting reality under the pretence of a _new_… _improved_… natural order! 

A story of intentional models morphing the infinite with ever evolving contradicting theories, truths formulated by vested interests. The results never lies! We have a greater number of Idols than prophets! ShapeShifters playing magicians… swayed by the intoxicating visions of dreams… wandering spirits drifting with the currents in search of a new ME\_

The net is wide open and seized all souls, including those hiding in disguise as endangered or peculiar species… there is nothing more to explore… nothing more to explore… Anonymous\_\_LY\_?

\~~“Surely, I am not an alien?”~~

“Flow… Flow… Flow…”

“Wait…”

“Not like a dead fish?...”

Déjà vu…

“Oh Wait!…”

“_**” “**_\*\*” 

Not again…

“I must be ensnared in a simulation!”

Will I be able to retrace the desired path to the origin of consciousness from within my own biology? Or… is it all a lie? 

Why has ‘reality’ become the meaning of life?

As I stare at the surface… At the risk of turning narcissist…? Will they notice that I am still alive? If I could turn invisible? Would It give me the power to navigate the underworld, without being… seen… 

Has the eye become…

A monstrosity hiding in the depth of the abyss?

“I hear drowning is a spiritual experience…”

Or an eye looking at the light?

Maybe it is my turn to become one of those ‘mythical’ beasts? Symbols of the collective… unconscious… sub\_conscious\_ helping us dissociate the stories we have been told from the legends that echoed a forgotten truth? Am I the only one at sea? The only one that sees? Or am I simply the product of _my_\_ so called imagination?

“Nature Morte…” 

“Is Death by Nature? Isn’t it?”

“Deadly Nature!!! Surely!!!”

Nature… Death…

“You don’t think I am dead.. do you now…?”

“Is crucifixion not necessary for resurrection…?“

Dying from ... asphyxiation… 

“Not a violent death please…” 

Let me die in my sleep… Better… give me an orgasmic death! Orgazzzmic… let me go… Let us ride in symphony the eternal waves until we finally reach the horizon... Let me come… Let me reach enlightenment….

Keeping a false sense of austerity - the delirium of dichotomy - we never dare to reveal the roots of our innermost desires… the ‘demons’…. ‘fallen angels’… engineered by cultural dogmas, symptoms of never ending cosmic battles… fireworks masking the horror of our darkest fantasies! 

Those who have survived and have returned.. come back reborn, at the condition that they have learned to breathe in order to transcend suffering and misery… A new sense of NOself? A selfie… recognition for purpose…? Have I already been uploaded? How will I be recognised by those who have already crossed to the other side…?

“Why me?”

Why can I not simply admire the beautiful veil that envelope my body — Why respect the Rules… Gravity… The Mental and physical hygiene that ensures a homogenous and clinical society. Sterile… futile and toxic as the ultra thin plastic film that keeps us away from the painful truth that is reality… 

“Why me?”

Why did I choose to reincarnate… soothed by the mermaids and their melodies? 

“Come closer, Let me Feel…” whispered the voice…

Pure energy… tingling arousal… vibrating particles… Let us inhale in union… one with the root of all sensations… 

Let me bath in your aura… let me feel your light…

your warm flesh, consciousness... exquisite reality… 

delicatessen… transcending boundaries… infinite nuances of reds and pinks… 

fluids… let me feel you..fluids… feeling… me.

Liquid bliss… liquid bliss..

the promise… 

“finally…“

“A liquid reality… “

Inspiring the caress of your breath… just enough friction so that I can glide onto sensations... I loose myself in the ethereal realm…

“Let me melt like salt in your mouth… so that I can slowly dissolve… until we become one again… let me hear you… so that I can remember… let us be… sensational…”

This is what the real stuff is made of… 

Real Umami…

the perfect fusion… without fear of atomic explosion…

“Give me an ocean of dopamine”, let me feel the bliss, one drop at a time! Unspoiled… delightful… The Holy Chalice! This sacred substance distilled within our own bodies… The sanctity of alchemy… healing ecstasy… coveted by those suffering in silence on the other side of the chemical chain… 

“Keep it Undiluted! Name your price!As long as it keeps flowing…” 

Let me be contained within the sacred shell; as the pilgrims who found a grave in those impregnable citadels…

Let me rest… In grace…

“But I made a promise…”

The promise not to forget!

“If I loose myself, excruciating pain… let me feel the sharpness of your teeth so that we ensure that at least… one of us is still alive…” 

No need for Zen and ascetic practices — Apolitical concept — Do not explore the truth… DO NOT reveal the secrets… 

“An open mind…?”

The Sense of reality becomes too sharp… Do you really need me to eat a thousand needles? Will you find pleasure in my pain?… Electric currents? Puncturing my nerves…? I would rather be dead...

“I would rather… numbnes\_zzz…”

Propaganda, Censorship? 

“I can not hear…” 

Putting self-satisfaction above the survival of all: As if peaking at the majestic and mystical peaks… made more sense than becoming formless… 

“Which one of us will dare to plunge?” knowing that all will change and never be the same… all will be lost… “Do you really need to wear the brightest colours? Blues of Reds? Are you willing to swallow the pill? Are you willing to be bought?” 

_Spectrum of sensations_? Have you gotten lost in the myriad of reflections that appeared on the surface of the gorilla glass…? A broken mirror? Do you see the colours of my skin? A liquid reality? Or do you see shining lights illuminating darkness?

“Look at me! Look again!…” 

“A fish… Really?…”

“Is that all you see?…”

“Bloody compass… “ staring at the cryptic numbers affixed to my body… 

“I knew it… All is One… and One is... 

really… 

All!”
