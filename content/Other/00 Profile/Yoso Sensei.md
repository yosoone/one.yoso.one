Yoso started his spiritual quest at an early age as influenced by his mother who is a natural therapist.

Initiated to meditation, yoga and alternative medicine in the early years of his life.

Learned the importance of past experiences, identity (the self), emotions, ideas and blockages & how to transform his thoughts through affirmations.

Treated for a big depression that turned into an awakening at 16 using Reiki and acupuncture. The early impact of Reiki at 16 transformed Yoso’s life which lead him to fine arts that he has been studying for 20 years.

Studied a variety of martial arts and hard mental physical techniques before discovering the power of chi gong & meditation.

Learned to develop his own personal style based on traditions & Intention.

The quest of being one with compassion and love lead Yoso to south japan where he now practice [Tattooing](https://tattoo.yoso.eu/), Reiki and the art of living in peace and harmony as a way of life. Reiki despite being soft and kind, being the most powerful technique he has had the chance to experience so far.

After trying the ‘hectic’ cosmopolitan ‘modern’ life, Yoso Moved away from the big cities to enjoy his life in the beautiful japanese country side where people are welcome for retreats, welcoming people for inspiration and to experience a simple yet beautiful way of life. Be the light and the changes you wish to see  in this world! The choices you make creates your reality, Follow your heart, trust your intuition! Live in Gratitude!

## Shinsei

Mindful Creation

Delving into the essence of mindfulness, a creative spirit finds grounding in the present moment. Every brushstroke, every line drawn, the materiality of the paper, the flow of ink and water, represents an act of profound meditation. The artist discovers peace and calmness in creation, fully immersing in the process. Art transcends being just a visual spectacle; it becomes a journey into the heart of mindfulness. It encourages viewers to pause, breathe, and engage with the present. Through their art, the artist not only creates but also nurtures a space for serene reflection—a visual meditation reminiscent of a Zen garden's stillness and depth. Harmony in nature, creation, reflection af a new found consciousness, inviting others toward a deeper appreciation of the present, fostering an inner connection that rises above the chaos of daily life.

## Tattoo

Transcend the self, _Soul,_ _Body_ and mind

Allowed me to explore Japanese culture, it’s dogmas.

Sour carver.

Through Practice and commitment, Yoso aims to create art that helps transcend the self, _Soul,_ _Body_ and mind.

Trained in the Arts from a young age, Yoso studied ‘Western’ traditions and the global contemporary pop/subcultures before developing a growing interest in Eastern philosophies. From Classical training to Digital and Political Activism, years of exploration and often paradoxical stimulations led him to Japan to pursue the [[Soul]]’s quest for inner peace and harmony.

Inspired by Nature, Shinto, Buddhism, Shaolin and a variety of methods and symbols issued from various spiritual traditions. Through Practice and commitment, Yoso aims to create art that helps transcend 'the self’, _Soul,_ _Body_ and mind.

Called Sensei by a few, Yoso knows that it is far too early to know the true meaning of wisdom, however, it is an encouragement to develop his practice that he can share with others.

_One breath at a time!_ _One bridge at a time_.

His love for people, the cultivation of spirit and a quest for integrity drew him to _the way of the ink_ also known as tattooing, the simplicity of intimate encounters, and the attention that is needed when working on a living medium. It requires a commitment to dissociate the truth from the noise.

Along with Tattooing and Irezumi, Yoso also pursues other creative practices such as Sumi-e, Digital Painting and photography - each medium bringing an opportunity to document and express various insights and experiences; [Reiki](http://reiki.yoso.eu/), mindfulness and meditation being the necessary foundations to cultivate the energy required to develop Inner peace and compassion.

Preparing every day before the dawn! Welcoming each sunrise with gratitude. Feeling the beautiful energy flowing within one’s body. Simplifying life and relationships, are what makes life worth living.

Yoso spends most of the Year in Miyazaki, Kyushu, Japan, with regular travels to Europe and occasionally other destinations.

If you wish to have a look at some of the tattoo work and inspirations, you can check the [tattoo portfolio](Tattoo.md), the small ‘practical’ guide regarding the tattoo [process](/tattooprocess), or the studio in [Miyazaki.](/miyazaki-tattoo-studio)

If you wish to hear what others have to say, you can read the [Testimonials](Testimonials.md), or dig a little deeper, into the role of the [_Japanese Tattoo today_](/japanese-tattoo) ‘Small Essay’ as perceived through a Horishi’s practice.

Artist, _Shokunin_, a life work in progress; Yoso is open to new collaborations and encounters! Whether relating to tattooing, [custom artwork](/customtattoodesign) commissions, Idea generations or consultations!

Previous collaborations include Nike, Diesel, Cao Fei, numerous festivals, artists, X-care in Taipei & The Serpentine Gallery in London to name a few!

Forever grateful for each encounter, and the patrons, friends and family for their integrity, love and support.

Life is a mystery. Let’s work together to bring more creativity and harmony to this reality.
