# Zazen Near Miyazaki

Temples in and around Miyazaki City (宮崎市) where anyone can join a regular zazen sitting (坐禅会, _zazenkai_). Call before a first visit: directory listings can fall behind a temple's actual schedule.[^1] [^4] [^6]

## Rinzai (臨済宗) — Myōshin-ji branch

All three are listed on Rinnō Net, the official site of the Rinzai and Ōbaku schools. All belong to the Myōshin-ji branch (妙心寺派), the same branch as Sogenji in Okayama, and all are free and open to anyone.[^1]

| Temple | Sitting | When | Address | Access | Tel. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Taiō-ji 泰翁寺** | 日曜坐禅会 (Sunday) | Every Sunday, 10:00 | 〒880-0121 宮崎市大字島之内7460-2 | JR Hyūga-Sumiyoshi Station, 15 min walk; or Miyakō bus stop Sumiyoshi, 10 min walk | 0985-39-3407 |
| **Uchiyama-ji 内山寺** | 木曜坐禅会 (Thursday) | Every Thursday, 19:00 | 〒889-1604 宮崎市清武町船引1592 | JR Kiyotake Station, 20 min walk | 0985-85-3775 |
| **Daikō-ji 大光寺** | 大光寺坐禅会 | 2nd and 4th Saturday, 19:00 | 〒880-0301 宮崎市佐土原町上田島767 | Bus from JR Sadowara Station toward Saito (via Matsushita), alight at Shinjō, 3 min walk | 0985-74-0345 |

- **Closest to the Sogenji pattern:** Taiō-ji — a weekly morning sitting in the same branch.
- **Weekday evening:** Uchiyama-ji on Thursdays.
- **Twice monthly:** Daikō-ji on Saturday evenings.

## Sōtō (曹洞宗)

No Sōtō temple in Miyazaki City could yet be confirmed as holding an open zazen sitting.

- **Official search.** The Sōtō school's portal, Sōtōzen Navi (曹洞禅ナビ), lists temples holding zazenkai, searchable by prefecture and purpose. Its Miyazaki results could not be retrieved directly; search it by hand.[^2]
- **Zenseiji 善栖寺**, 〒880-0905 宮崎市中村西1-5-3, tel. 0985-51-3953, is a Sōtō temple in the city. Its official listing does not show a zazenkai, so ask by phone.[^3]
- The Sōtō school itself advises confirming date, time and dress with the temple by phone before joining any sitting for the first time.[^4]

## Online

The Rinzai Young Monks' Association (臨済宗青年僧の会) runs online zazen sessions with a published calendar — useful between temple visits.[^5]
