“Compassion compels us to apply every single skilful method available in our effort to help them, which means we must pretend to teach. By teaching, we can offer the person dreaming about a snake the choice of many kinds of stick to drive it away with; and by doing so, we serve the Buddhadharma. Our sole reason for teaching can only ever be the supreme objective of unconditional compassion. And if, out of compassion, we apply the skilful method of teaching, the light of the Buddha, Dharma and Saṅgha will continue to shine.” (“The Sūtra of The Teaching of Vimalakīrti”, 2017, p. 20)

The [[mantra]], probably the essence of what I have learned from [[Buddhism]]. The Main centre of [[concentration]] and attention! The reference points for my thoughts and [[actions]]!
