**Kishu Busshin**

Kishu Busshin is a coined phrase taken from Buddhist teachings that is also a statement of medical ethics. Kishu, the "demon's hand," refers to <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">the hand of the medical professional holding a scalpel</mark>, <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">while Busshin, the "Buddha's heart," refers to the desire to heal patients</mark>. It is because it has the benevolent heart of a Buddha that the demon's hand inserting the scalpel calmly is radiant as a thing of value. We see a company's earning of profits as the demon's hand and its return of those profits to society as its Buddha's heart, and in our efforts to maintain the optimal balance between the two, we will contribute to the growth and prosperity of society.
