The Union of Appearance and Emptiness.
Things are there and not there at the same time.

Reality and everything that is perceived is like the reflection of the moon on the water. It is there, but it is not there.

Whether confident or experiencing a lack of attention. Both aspects always happen simultaneously.

Everything we experience is that way.

All the methods, from Japan, The tea ceremony, the Zen garden, The Tibetan temples and rituals. All are ways to express this simple truth,

"gom", the word that is used in Tibetan and translated to meditation in english, means that any method that breaks the shell of duality, out of the box and immediatly destroy the next box.

This is what is considered the spiritual path 8  Especially in Mahayanna buddhism.

all methods bring you to [[Mindfulness]]

Anything that is getting you closer to the truth of non-duality is the path!
