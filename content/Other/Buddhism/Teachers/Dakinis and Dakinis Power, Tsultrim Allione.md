# Transcript

It's funny because when somebody asks me what is a dakini, I always go blank, which I think is actually interesting in terms of what a dakini is.

Dakinis and Dakini Power Because a dakini takes you out of words and logos into a direct experience, and so to define the dakini in a way is impossible, because she is beyond that and she takes you beyond that.

Given that, "dakini" means "sky-goer," or "mkha'" \[Tib.] is "sky" and "'gro" \[Tib.] is "go," and "ma" is the feminine marker.  So they say that she's called that because she moves through space or through emptiness.

She's like a messenger of emptiness.\
Dakinis can be human beings and they can also be deities, wisdom deities.\
And there are also worldly dakinis who are not human and they're not wisdom dakinis, but they're kinds of spirits, I guess you could say, that may be partially enlightened but not entirely.

So there's lots of different kinds of dakinis.

What I focus on in my work is the five wisdom dakinis, who are enlightened, they're equal to Buddhas in terms of their understanding.

Dakinis are fierce, not always but generally they have a fierce aspect or maybe semi-wrathful aspect.  To me, that fierce energy is interesting because of the way the fierce feminine has been denied.  That if you're a fierce woman, you're dangerous.\
You become the witch or the bitch.

And so the way that dakinis have that fierceness and clarity and wisdom, and I guess you could say allow that aspect of the feminine psyche but in a transformed form, in wisdom which is always based in compassion and an understanding of emptiness,then you have that.

So I think the simple way to say what a dakini is, is a conductor or a mediator or a midwife for wisdom.
