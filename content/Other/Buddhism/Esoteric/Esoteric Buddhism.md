# Esoteric Buddhism

Esoteric Buddhism is the family of Buddhist traditions built on the tantras: ritual identification with a buddha through body, speech and mind, worked with mudrā, mantra and mandala. It arose in India and spread in two main branches: north into Tibet as **Vajrayāna**, and east through Tang China to Japan as **Shingon** (and Tendai esotericism). For how the two branches relate, and why "Japanese Vajrayāna" is misleading, see [[Shingon and Vajrayana]].
