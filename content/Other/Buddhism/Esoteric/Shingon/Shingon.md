# Shingon

_Shingon_ (真言, "true word," a rendering of _mantra_) is the esoteric school that [[Kukai|Kūkai]] (空海, 774–835) brought to Japan from Tang China in 806, with its mountain centre at [[Koyasan|Kōya-san]]. It rests on two scriptures, the _Mahāvairocana Sūtra_ (大日経) and the _Vajraśekhara_ (金剛頂経), and the two mandalas drawn from them, the Womb Realm and the Diamond Realm. Its aim is _sokushin jōbutsu_ (即身成仏), buddhahood in this very body.

Shingon is a sibling of Tibetan Vajrayāna, not an offshoot of it. See [[Shingon and Vajrayana]].
