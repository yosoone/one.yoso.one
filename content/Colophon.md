# Colophon

This site is the public face of my Obsidian vault, a working knowledge base of notes, references, past projects and unfinished ideas. It is written by **Yoso**, in Miyazaki, Japan.

## How it is made

- **Writing:** [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md), in plain Markdown.
- **Publishing:** [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz) v5, hosted on Cloudflare Pages at [www.yoso.one](https://www.yoso.one).
- **Opt-in only:** a note appears here only when its `publish` property is `true`; everything else stays private.
- **Feed:** an RSS feed is generated automatically.[^1]

## AI disclosure

Some notes are drafted or structured with the help of Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. Notes where AI was involved carry the property `AI: true`. I review and edit them before publishing.
